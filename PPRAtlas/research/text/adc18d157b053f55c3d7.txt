CONCLUSION
T
HE trophic structure in the Sine-Saloum estuary
is changing mainly from decline of l'ish stocks
due to intensification of fishing activity and envi­

tem stability and resources sustainability.
Future management policies should consider re­
source conservation as such system serve as nursery
