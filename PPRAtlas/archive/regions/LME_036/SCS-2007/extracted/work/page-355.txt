6.1.25. Sharks and rays
Elasmobranchs were divided into demersal and pelagic groups. Demersal sharks
and rays represented about 0.1% of bottom trawlers' catches in the late 1980s. This was
assumed to represent the relative abundance of demersal elasmobranchs relative to the
total demersal biomass in the late 1990s. Thus biomass was estimated to be about 0.001
t- km" . However, this biomass level was found to be too low to support the fishery. Thus
