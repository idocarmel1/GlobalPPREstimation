Downloads are generated from the embedded, verified data. Selected records include validity reasons and diagnostics; selected JSON also includes group coefficients.

SC keeps H as catch, with no added internal return. SM removes D from catch and adds it once to M0, adjusting EE and detritus closure. SE routes D to a distinct external sink without invented migration. SR is available only for the original Humboldt reference with verified destination evidence.

Primary-production ratios are suppressed when an all-source numerator includes imported support but the denominator contains only internal producers. Native currency and missing area/time normalization remain explicit. The original /9 carbon convention is an assumption shared with the first experiment.

Displayed model group counts include the initializer's import-support group; original source group counts are in the screening catalog. At 100% discard, some finite flow-based coefficients become extremely large as the remaining removal approaches zero. These endpoint values are sensitive to source rounding and are stress diagnostics, not robust ecological forecasts; a zero retained-catch footprint does not validate the coefficient.
