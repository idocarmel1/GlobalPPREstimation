# 28_646_Guinea_(1998)

This model is a geographic transfer to LME_028, not a model demonstrated to cover the Guinea Current LME. The source describes 111,932 km² off the country Guinea, larger than its EEZ (Guénette & Diallo pp.124-125). A mapping percentage measures label assignment only and does not establish spatial representativeness.

[Source PDF](https://www.seaaroundus.org/doc/publications/books-and-reports/2004/Palomares-et-al-west-africa-ecosystems.pdf). The complete archived PDF was searched; Table 6 (printed p.131, PDF p.135) was visually inspected and transcribed with the original group numbering. Table 9 was compared numerically. Table 14 was inspected as a historical fishery allocation, not universal membership.

The inherited member file was incomplete. Its Table 7 entries were diet-study examples, often from other regions, and could not be promoted to direct membership. The full Table 6 now supplies 263 fish-member rows after exact deduplication, plus the source's explicit Cephalopoda definition (264 total). Printed names are preserved, genus initials expanded, and accepted names use WoRMS responses archived beside the mapping. Solitas gruveli is explicitly present in two groups; that ambiguity is preserved. The source's Mulets pool contains non-mullets, while Mugil curema is in Dem M inv. Major tunas include billfish. Table 6 placements control all such records.

Source verification found 13 B/EE entries outside strict printed rounding in the Ecobase JSON, principally EE differences below 0.002 and biomass differences below 0.001. Consumer PB and QB match printed precision. The source Oiseaux TL is 3.77 versus 3.545 in the imported result. These are provenance/version differences, not silently corrected parameters; see source-check.json. The source itself stresses approximate diet and catch/discard reconstruction. Input mass balance is a computational check, not proof of source fidelity.

No completed historical A/B arm was found. A new isolated source-only baseline was produced from the untouched catch stub and Table 6 using exact/accepted names and genus unions, before applying the production inference rules. It is a bounded deterministic ablation, not a blinded independent-agent test, not a comparison of the two full skills, and not evidence that combining the skills improves accuracy. See comparison.txt: source-only coverage is 52.2%, versus the completed mapping below; group-set agreement is 53.6% of tonnage. The primary difference is the 32.3% of catch labelled Marine fishes not identified, apportioned across explicit coastal/shelf residual guilds. No PPR-method comparison or temporal/spatial validation is implied. The comparison tool counts composite_split separately, so its documented-evidence fraction does not equal member-validation coverage.

No separate supplement was identified in the chapter or archive; the source inventory and membership are contained in the report. Original source pages remain archived. Taxonomy service endpoint: https://www.marinespecies.org/rest/AphiaRecordsByNames (queried 2026-09-07).
