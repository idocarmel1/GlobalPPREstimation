How to report these results
Use landings-only PPR as the central atlas estimate, retaining reported and unreported landings. Keep total catch, discarded catch and their coverage visible as separate accounting information.
A discard-routing band applies compatible perturbed coefficients to the same landed catch vector. It does not add discarded-catch PPR to the upper endpoint. Keep the selected annual NPP denominator fixed for every endpoint.
Call the range a discard-routing sensitivity envelope, not a confidence interval. Show unassessed combinations explicitly; a few tested food webs do not establish global uncertainty bounds.
Keep NPP, transfer efficiency, biological parameters, unidentified-catch treatment, source conversion and geographical transfer as distinct uncertainties. A routing-invariant benchmark does not prove that recycling uncertainty is zero.
