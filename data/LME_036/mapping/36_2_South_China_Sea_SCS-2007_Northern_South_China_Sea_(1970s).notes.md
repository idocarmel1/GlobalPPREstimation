# Northern South China Sea, 1970s: source audit

This is the northern continental shelf, mainly within the Chinese EEZ, at depths below 200 m (Cheung 2007 printed p.170 / PDF p.185; 106 degrees 53 minutes-119 degrees 48 minutes E, 17 degrees 10 minutes-25 degrees 52 minutes N). Catch is for the entire South China Sea LME. Mapping coverage does not establish geographic representativeness. Both periods are selected as usable in data/model_selection.xlsx; its preference for the 2000s is retained without merging the models.

[Cheung (2007), UBC doctoral thesis](https://open.library.ubc.ca/collections/ubctheses/831/items/1.0074894), DOI 10.14288/1.0074894, is the direct source. The archived 369-page PDF was inspected: final Tables 6.1/6.2 establish exact groups/catches; Appendix 6.1, printed pp.322-341 / PDF pp.337-356, provides the definitions and explicitly named examples. Important croaker size and stanza pages were visually checked. Species examples from the prose were transcribed before mapping; no complete species-per-group inventory was found. No separate supplement was needed or claimed. The existing extraction SOURCE_CONFLICTS.md records numerical discrepancies between appendix and final tables; this pass did not alter those inputs.

The group structure combines taxonomic families, residual habitat/size pools, and linked juvenile/adult stages. Source membership outranks SAU class: Acetes belongs to Zooplankton (p.323), Squillidae/stomatopods to Benthic crustaceans (pp.324-325), and Pennahia to small Croakers despite SAU medium size (p.333). Lobsters remain in Benthic crustaceans because its definition excludes shrimps and crabs. Harpadon is placed by the source's explicit Synodontidae definition, supported by [FishBase family classification](https://www.fishbase.se/summary/Harpadon_nehereus.html); this is a taxonomic extension, not an explicit named member. Stromateidae does not silently include the carangid Parastromateus niger. Its generic benthopelagic assignment is low confidence.

The source hairtail group is split at 18 months. Large croakers use two stages divided at 24 months; large demersal and pelagic fishes use two stages with adult stage from 18 months. Species-level unaged catches use model_catch weights over those stages. These fixed weights are period-specific: the 2000s catches are juvenile-heavy and 1970s catches adult-heavy. They remain constant over the 1950-2019 catch series; no interpolated time-varying selectivity is introduced. The exact source table catches, rather than rounded prose percentages, set the proportions. Coarse Sciaenidae and residual size-ambiguous aggregates include all supported size/stage pools at low confidence.

Two source limitations remain explicit. Appendix pp.337-338 separately describes small/large benthopelagic fish, but the final model has only one Benthopelagic fish row; its size composition is unresolved, so inferred assignments there are low confidence. Appendix p.339 says demersal in the first sentence of Large pelagic fish, evidently inconsistent with its heading, stanza names and scombrid examples; those consistent elements determine habitat. Adult groupers has no separate juvenile grouper row; its family definition covers the available grouper catch.

Oceanic billfishes and unsupported deep-pelagic fish remain Unresolved; no coastal juvenile/adult SPPR is forced onto them. The 12 unresolved taxa total 820,180 tonnes, led by Ruvettus pretiosus and Xiphias gladius. Per-taxon reasons and full precision are in the sibling review.json.

**99.7884% of 387,582,302 tonnes is mapped.** All 374 rows have decisions: high 128, medium 186, low 48, unresolved 12. The high/medium/low tonnage split is approximately 35.7% / 35.1% / 29.0%; headline coverage includes all three and must not be presented as source confirmation. Source member checking finds zero contradictions. Coarse composites use catch composition unless an explicit model_catch basis is recorded; the latter preserves stage selectivity even where no independent catch record identifies a stage.

The taxonomy CSV covers all 38 source JSON groups with exact sequence/name checks. The group dictionary also describes the derived diet_import workbook row. Only taxon_descr was changed in JSON, and the named SPPR workbook was refreshed through tools/run_sppr.py. Algorithm and Jensen calculations remain unchanged. Existing GE/TE warnings and group-level numerical limitations must be carried into final results; With Egestion is OK in model_health. Final workbook building and numerical verification belong to integration.

Final regeneration verification compared all three SPPR sheets (all, inner and primary-producer contributions) against the committed workbook: every deterministic column and its availability are identical. Exact source group names, all taxonomy descriptions and JSON numerical fields were checked; JSON changes are limited to taxon_descr. Monte Carlo outputs vary between runs; the sibling regeneration-comparison.json records their differences. Regeneration used PYTHONUTF8=1 and python -X utf8 because the model loader otherwise uses Windows cp1255 and corrupts Unicode size symbols. The final workbook passed the exact-name checks; no algorithm change was made.
