# Guinea 1998 mapping: independent combined-skill arm

This is a mapping-stage comparison artifact for the 112,000 km² model off the Republic of Guinea. It is not a model of the entire Guinea Current LME or of Equatorial Guinea. Applying its 1998 group SPPR to LME-wide catch for 1950-2019 is a spatial and temporal extrapolation, even for a high-confidence taxonomic join.

Source: Guénette, S. and Diallo, I. (2004), “Addendum: Modèles de la côte guinéenne, 1985 et 1998”, pp.124-159 in Palomares and Pauly (eds.), *West African marine ecosystems: models and fisheries impacts*, FCRR 12(7). The archived [volume](https://www.seaaroundus.org/doc/publications/books-and-reports/2004/Palomares-et-al-west-africa-ecosystems.pdf) contains the required member table (Table 6 p.131/PDF p.135), habitat definitions (p.127), and invertebrate definitions (pp.125-126). No separate supplement was needed or identified for this mapping; no additional supplement was downloaded. The supplied audited members, taxonomy and group dictionary were reused without alteration. Exact group strings come from the copied 1998 groups_df workbook.

The group axis combines taxonomy, body size, feeding guild and depth/habitat. Côtier denotes mainly <40 m, Dem generally 20-200 m, and Bathy >200 m. L/M/S are relative body-size classes; they are not assumed to have the same cutoffs as SAU. Pred means fish/cephalopod-dominated diet and inv means primarily benthic/planktonic invertebrates. Named commercial groups coexist with guild pools; Mulets also contains non-mullet herbivores. The source explicitly includes oceanic tuna/billfish and deep-water fish groups. A generic “coastal model” exclusion of those taxa would be wrong for this model.

Model selection says usable=yes. The copied model_health reports balanced input, diet rows summing to one, and no EE issues. GE and With Egestion are OK; TE is WARN, with convergence and balance nevertheless true and two recorded warnings. This arm changes no biological parameters, runs no SPPR, and makes no new claim about whole-model fitness.

Evidence rules: an exact printed or supplied accepted name takes precedence over SAU habitat/size. Genus/family extrapolations from regional members are medium confidence, not exact source membership. Ecological analogues are low confidence unless stronger evidence is documented. Source species exceptions override a broad family entry; Scorpaenidae therefore includes named shelf/deep members even though the table also gives a coastal family entry. The source duplicate Solitas gruveli placement is retained in applicable broad flatfish candidate sets and is not silently repaired.

Composite weights are fixed for the whole catch period. Blank weights request the combined skill's identified-catch-composition basis, with fallback model_catch, model_biomass, then equal if the builder has no support. These weights are a proxy based on candidate-group catch and may be contaminated by unrelated species sharing a guild; they are not measured within-family proportions. Explicit model_biomass is used for unresolved habitat/depth/size mixtures. Biomass is not fishery selectivity, and these allocations need sensitivity analysis before scientific use. No arbitrary equal fractions or fabricated composition percentages are supplied.

Highest-impact judgement: unidentified medium-demersal finfish is spread over generic coastal and shelf demersal guilds, with dedicated commercial stocks, oceanic, bathy and elasmobranch groups excluded. That candidate restriction follows the combined playbook but is an assumption about an unidentified pile, not demonstrated taxonomic composition. Perciformes includes documented commercial perch-like stocks that the taxonomic label can contain. These broad residual rows are low confidence.

Sarotherodon melanotheron is a low-confidence functional analogue of the coastal herbivorous Mulets pool, based on its shallow brackish habitat and detrital diet; it is not asserted to be a mullet. Pellonula leonensis is also a low-confidence marine/brackish small-pelagic analogue, with freshwater catch potentially unrepresented. In contrast, S. galilaeus remains unresolved because its freshwater/brackish record lacks a demonstrated marine food-web pathway. The Chondrichthyes label is unresolved because its explicit chimaera component cannot be separated quantitatively from the supported elasmobranch component. These are conservative decisions, not zeros.

Focused external checks, consulted 2026-09-07:

- [NCBI Scyris alexandrina](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/Taxonomy/Browser/wwwtax.cgi?id=3049903): connects the catch name with the source Alectis spelling; recorded as synonym evidence rather than changing the supplied members file.
- [FishBase blackchin tilapia](https://www.fishbase.org/summary/Sarotherodon_melanotheron.html), [mango tilapia](https://www.fishbase.org/summary/Sarotherodon-galilaeus), and [Pellonula](https://www.fishbase.org/summary/1536): support explicit salinity/feeding limitations.
- [FAO Pentheroscion sheet](https://www.fao.org/4/ag419e/ag419e03.pdf) and [FishBase jellynose](https://www.fishbase.se/Fieldguide/FieldGuideSummary.php?c_code=710&genusname=Guentherus&speciesname=altivela): support targeted shelf/deep predator analogies.
- [WoRMS Holocephali](https://www.marinespecies.org/aphia.php?id=10196&p=taxdetails): confirms chimaeras are outside Elasmobranchii.
- [FishBase Inermiidae](https://www.fishbase.se/summary/FamilySummary.php?id=479&lang=french): western-Atlantic distribution supports leaving the family record unresolved pending identity review.
- [FAO North Atlantic sharks catalogue](https://www.fao.org/4/i3178e/i3178e.pdf): includes Centroscyllium fabricii from Sierra Leone; this prevents an unsupported high-latitude-only exclusion and supports a low-confidence medium-deep analogue.

All catch taxa retain their input metadata and original order. The 1985 mapping was not requested and was not completed; prepare_mapping's extra empty stub was moved aside. The independent mapping work used only the isolated arm evidence and public sources, not the production crosswalk or another agent's decisions. Shared audited membership evidence means this is not a fully blinded experiment. Validator PASS establishes file consistency and coverage, not scientific accuracy or statistical non-inferiority.
