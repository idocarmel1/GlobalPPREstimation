# 28_646_Guinea_(1998)

Axis: taxonomy and commercial single stocks nested within size, feeding and depth/habitat pools.
Prefixes: L/G means large; M medium; S/P small; inv invertebrate feeders; pred piscivorous/cephalopod predators. Coastal groups occupy chiefly <40 m, demersal groups 20-200 m, bathyal groups >200 m (source p.128). These are ecological assemblages, not duplicate geographic copies that require every taxon to be split across them.
Area: 111,932 km² off the country Guinea, extending beyond its EEZ, with 43% shallower than 200 m (source pp.124-125, Table 1). It is not a measured model of the entire Guinea Current LME. Any LME_028 PPR built from this mapping is a geographic transfer until overlap and representativeness are established.
Period: 1998; 44 source groups plus algorithm diet_import. The separate 1985/726 model was not combined with this model.
Catch-capable: fish, cephalopods, crustaceans and benthos. Exclude mammals, turtles, birds, zooplankton, PP, detritus and import from catch inference.
Membership: full Table 6 transcribed; accepted names checked with WoRMS. Tables 2/4 and prose supply non-fish definitions. Table 7 contains diet examples from other regions and was not treated as a member inventory. Table 14 is an allocation of 1982-1984 trawl catches, not a replacement for Table 6 biological membership.
Key traps: Mulets includes some non-mullets and excludes Mugil curema, which Table 6 puts in Dem M inv. Major tunas include billfish; source membership controls these records. Solitas gruveli occurs in two source groups and remains explicitly ambiguous.
Model health: source parameters largely match Table 9 at printed precision; Oiseaux TL is 3.77 in the table versus 3.545 in the imported model, with its PB/QB matching. Diet rows balance but the source's coarse diets and catch reconstruction remain limitations.
