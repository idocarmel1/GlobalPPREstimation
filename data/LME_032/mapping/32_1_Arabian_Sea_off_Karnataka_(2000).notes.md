# Arabian Sea off Karnataka, 2000: complete source re-derivation

This is a **27,000 km2 Karnataka shelf model** applied to catch for the entire Arabian Sea LME. Mapping coverage measures the taxonomic join, not geographic representativeness. The model is selected as usable in `data/model_selection.xlsx`.

[CMFRI Bulletin 51 (2005)](https://eprints.cmfri.org.in/3945/), archived as `ARAB-2005/CMFRI-Bulletin-51-88a9c885.pdf`, is the direct membership source. The two-column Components of Ecological Groupings table, printed pp.11-12 / PDF pp.21-22, was visually inspected. All **129 numbered members** were transcribed, preserving printed spellings. Group accounts, printed pp.14-20, corroborate ecological definitions. Authoritative model strings `Skates & Rays` and `Micro Nekton` remain exact despite the source's `Stakes & Rays` and `Nekton`. No separate supplement was needed; existing text extracts were locators rather than authority on table placement.

The bulletin's p.17 and [CMFRI black-pomfret account](https://eprints.cmfri.org.in/7854/1/B139.pdf) reconcile Formio niger with Parastromateus niger. The [NCBI squid record with WoRMS links](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/Taxonomy/Browser/wwwtax.cgi?id=78425&mode=Info) corroborates Loligo/Uroteuthis. The alias column is a limited matching aid, not an exhaustive modern nomenclatural revision. Caranx kalla is preserved without asserting an uncertain synonym or treating it as evidence for every modern Caranx.

Every assignment was independently re-derived from membership, source definitions or an explicit ecological inference. **No inherited_mapping row remains.** The previous assignment was not an input to the new decision rules. The re-derivation changed 101 group expressions; exact before/after assignments and tonnage are in `source-review/32_1_Arabian_Sea_off_Karnataka_(2000).changes.csv`. The previous notes remain in `source-review/previous-notes.md` only as historical evidence, with superseded claims clearly confined there.

Source placements explicitly retained include Thryssa in Clupeids, Bregmaceros in Anchovies, jellyfish in Micro Nekton, Plotosus/Fistularia in Benthic Omnivores, Parastromateus in Med Benthic Carnivores, and ponyfishes/Lactarius in Small Benthic Carnivores. Documented membership takes precedence over SAU habitat/size. Unlisted lineage extensions and diet/habitat analogues are identified separately and receive reduced confidence.

Coarse Carangidae, Engraulidae, Siluriformes, Clupeiformes and flatfishes include every documented relevant compartment. Separately identified species catch does not prove that those species are absent from an unidentified aggregate. Their explicit constant weights, and those for Scombridae, use only identified catch from the same family/order; unrelated catfish or croaker catch cannot set carangid or flatfish shares. Other composites use catch composition with the builder's recorded fallback. Generic demersal aggregates include Benthic Omnivores and are low confidence because their diet composition is unknown.

A final species-level check overrides the misleading SAU <30 cm label for longnose trevally: [FishBase](https://fishbase.se/summary/Platycaranx-chrysophrys) documents common 65 cm and coastal open-water adults, supporting a medium-confidence Large Benthopelagics analogue. [Flavocaranx bajad](https://www.fishbase.se/summary/Carangoides-bajad.html), common 42 cm, receives the same analogy. Remaining unlisted carangid analogues are low confidence because model size classes do not equal SAU classes. [Ferdauia orthogrammus](https://www.fishbase.se/Country/CountrySpeciesSummary.php?c_code=508&id=1909) feeds on sandy-bottom crustaceans and is a low-confidence Med Benthic Carnivores analogue. Family weights were recomputed from the updated identified carangid catch.

Billfishes, unrepresented deep-ocean taxa and reef herbivore/omnivore groups without an established guild match remain Unresolved. Harpadon nehereus remains the largest individual analogue, at low confidence: the source lists Saurida but not Bombay-duck, and benthopelagic habitat is a material alternative. It is not described as a verified member.

**98.7301% of 197,852,454 tonnes is mapped** over 1950-2019. All 430 rows have decisions: high 217, medium 128, low 43, unresolved 42. Unresolved catch is 2,512,590 tonnes (1.2699%). Sixteen composites carry 25.5% of catch. The source member checker confirms 66 taxa carrying 28.8% of catch and finds zero contradictions; remaining coverage relies on containment or visible inference. Largest unresolved labels are Bramidae, Siganidae, Anguilliformes and Monacanthidae, followed by billfishes. Anguilliformes spans feeding guilds whose proportions cannot be established. Exact amounts and per-taxon reasons are in `*.review.json`.

The dictionary covers all workbook groups and the taxonomy CSV all 24 source JSON groups. The derived `diet_import` workbook row is not a source JSON group. Only taxon_descr was changed in the JSON; the named SPPR workbook was regenerated through the repository wrapper. Algorithm and Jensen calculations remain unchanged. GE/egestion health is OK; TE retains its near-singular warning. Monte Carlo sampling can produce regenerated differences. Final PPR building and numerical result verification are the integration step.

Final regeneration verification compared all three SPPR sheets (all, inner and primary-producer contributions) against the committed workbook: every deterministic column and its availability are identical. Exact source group names, all taxonomy descriptions and JSON numerical fields were checked; JSON changes are limited to taxon_descr. Monte Carlo outputs vary between runs; the sibling regeneration-comparison.json records their differences.
