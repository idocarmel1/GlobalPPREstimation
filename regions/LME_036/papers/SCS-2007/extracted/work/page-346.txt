6.1.14. Snappers
Members of this group are from the family Lutjanidae. Biomass estimates were
not available for the 1970s and 2000s. Thus biomasses of snappers in the 1970s and
2000s models were estimated by assuming EE (= 0.95). Natural mortality rate was
estimated from Pauly's empirical equation (Pauly 1980). Since independent estimates of
fishing mortality were not available, I assumed that the group was under similar
exploitation rate (E = F/Z) as the other exploited demersal fish groups. Based on the
average E of 0.62 for demersal fish groups in the N S C S (South China Sea Fisheries
Institute, unpublished data) and substituting Z = F+M into the equation, total mortality
was roughly estimated to be 1.75 year" . Populations of snappers was assumed to be
