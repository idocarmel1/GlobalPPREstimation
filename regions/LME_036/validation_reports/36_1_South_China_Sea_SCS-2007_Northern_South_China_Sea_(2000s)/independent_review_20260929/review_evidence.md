## Mapping review decisions

Source definitions were read directly in Appendix 6.1. The current model taxonomy and workbook evidence give the exact group membership context. High denotes explicit source membership, verified synonym, or an unambiguous taxonomic definition; named representatives do not automatically expand a group. Single-group allocation is W1. M5 retains a supported ecological inference, with the SAU class as a proxy and its limitation stated. Broad residual family/genus assignments are conservatively Low when one representative size or habitat band does not establish containment of all members. The final single Benthopelagic fish pool remains Low because Appendix 6.1 separately describes small and large pools without resolving the final aggregation.

All 135 recorded stage-proxy proportions were independently compared with canonical group.export and saved source catches; every difference is below 1e-10 (`state_comparison.json`). Thirty mixed non-stage or broad-category decisions are Unresolved in this review where complete catch composition lacks defensible evidence; stored values are retained in JSON but report weights are ?. ## Geography

R is the Sea Around Us LME036 marine polygon; S is the coast-to-broken-line area in Cheung Figure 6.1, printed p.170 / PDF p.185, the same northern shelf domain as both thesis time periods. The two saved figures embedded in the researcher record were inspected alongside the original PDF page. Visual landmarks are the Gulf of Tonkin, Hainan, the Chinese coast, Taiwan and the offshore broken line. Reading the geographic grid on R and transferring those landmarks gives A approximately 15–25% and B approximately 90–100%. These deliberately broad visual ranges are not polygon measurements or statistical confidence intervals. The shelf strip occupies about one fifth of R; almost all of S lies in R, with the main mismatch at the northeast edge near Taiwan. Land is excluded conceptually; no raw pixel area ratio or bounding-box area was used. Map-scale differences and the ungridded source boundary limit precision. The current template permits this approximate assessment, replacing the old prohibition on visual estimates. No map or boundary output was changed.
