# LME_034 validation evidence

Validation 30 September 2026; selected 1978 Bay of Bengal model. This review reassessed all 315 exact catch labels and adopted 35 candidate-set corrections, 245 allocation changes and 9 overall-confidence changes. It reused adequate saved group coefficients and retained classic/catch/NPP inputs, ran one direct diagnostic call for each default GE/TE/With Egestion configuration, and recalculated dependent annual outputs across the retained years, catch bases, scopes and unidentified treatments.

Source fidelity remains unverified. Published diet omissions are normalized in the canonical input and the constructor derives biomass accumulation to close that transformed input. No supported author-native diet repair was available; canonical model JSON was kept unchanged. Model-derived results are provisional and production eligibility is false. Independent 2019 landings simple-chain PPR remains 379,324,412.0465857 tonnes C.

## Provenance and limits

The baseline workbook is a frozen copy made before adoption; its source and canonical hashes are retained in baseline_evidence.json. The original 62-page December 2013 Guénette report is retained in the region paper archive and is distinct from the later 69-page 2014 distributed edition. Source-page text/images are extracts from that PDF. Mapping decisions, independent geographic analysis, diagnostic reopening checks, arithmetic and layout verification were created by this validation, not by the original author. The regional MODEL_PROFILE/taxonomy/membership files are identical copies of the adopted mapping-review evidence. The master Word template and LME_036 researcher edits were preserved. Temporary builders, rendered PDFs/page images and artifact-tool logs remain outside final deliverables.

Source-diagnostics/evidence_index.json and source_completeness.json explicitly retain the missing author_native_balanced_model role. Mapping and diagnostic indices verify retained identities and files independently. The root index includes final integration and actual browser reconciliation. Its only missing required role is the author-native balanced model; this is a scientific source-fidelity limitation, not an omitted validation step.
