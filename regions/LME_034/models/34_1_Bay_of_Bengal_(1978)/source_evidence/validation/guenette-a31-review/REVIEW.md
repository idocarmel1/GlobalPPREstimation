# Guénette 2013: Appendix A3.1 versus LME_034 catch mapping

Review date: 2026-09-10. Production mappings and model results were not changed.

## Scope and interpretation

A3.1, pp.50–53, is titled "Source of fish diets". It identifies species whose diet studies inform groups; it is not the exhaustive membership list. A1.1, pp.37–41, allocates catch names; A1.3, pp.43–45, describes group composition. All four A3.1 pages were rendered and visually inspected.

Species names were extracted from the first two scientific-name tokens and deduplicated across repeated studies; the printed Tylosurus crocodilus crocodilus entry was reduced to its binomial. Catch labels were compared at exact species-name level, plus the confirmed Scyris indica / Alectis indica name change. Genus/family records were not treated as proof that a particular listed species was caught.

Scyris indica correspondence: [WoRMS](https://www.marinespecies.org/aphia.php?p=taxlist&tName=Alectis+indica), checked 2026-09-10. No genus-level fuzzy matches were used. Makaira mazara and Makaira nigricans are retained separately, as in A1.3; their taxonomic treatment differs among authorities, so this audit does not merge the author's distinct model assignments.

Agreement means consistency of the assigned biological group with these source sections. It does not validate estimated regional shares, unknown catch composition, or all scientific assumptions in the model.

## Results

- Unique species binomials in A3.1: 156.
- Distinct catch labels: 315; years 1950–2019.
- Exact catch-name matches: 52; confirmed name-change matches: 1.
- Total matched catch labels: 53, carrying 12.7488% of all-period catch.
- Catch labels absent as individual species from A3.1 after that name reconciliation: 262, carrying 87.2512%.
- For the matched set: 51 biological-group agreements, plus two justified differences (yellowfin and bigeye tuna). No unexplained biological-group disagreement found in this comparison.
- Catch-series totals differ from the stored resolved mapping totals by at most 0.00705412 tonnes per taxon. This review computes shares directly from the catch-series CSV; it does not claim bitwise identity between saved products.

Absence from A3.1 does not imply absence from A1.1 or A1.3. Nor does it by itself require weights. A regional split can be required even for an explicitly named species.

- Represented in A3.1: No split: 20; Regional split only: 33.
- Absent from A3.1: Multiple biological groups (and regions where applicable): 20; Regional split only: 186; No split: 56.
