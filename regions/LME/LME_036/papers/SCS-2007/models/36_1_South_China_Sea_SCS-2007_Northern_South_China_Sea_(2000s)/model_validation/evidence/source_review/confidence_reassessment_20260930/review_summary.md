## Lessons integrated in existing skills

- Use complete operative group definitions and competing-group exclusions; unnamed species can qualify for High when all criteria are verified.
- Separate membership in a stage-pair union from uncertainty about the within-pair split.
- Distinguish species maximum length from common/individual length; preserve TL/FL/SL.
- Check provider reporting scope, relevant regional exceptions and historical taxonomic concepts.
- Use detailed habitat behavior and distinguish model applicability from functional membership.
- Reproduced proxy weights and source-assumed percentages are not measured catch composition.
- Review existing strong ratings as well as weak ones; a confidence reassessment can lower ratings.
- As subsequently requested, allow evidenced mappings from geographically nearby models when no better focal mapping can be made; crosswalk group definitions and assess transfer confidence (M12), keeping weights and coefficients separate.

These lessons are integrated in the existing unified paper-to-PPR entry point and taxonomy/regional/allocation references, the existing validation skill, and the template filling guide. No new skill was created.
