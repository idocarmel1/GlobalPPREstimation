# Current adopted taxon record fields

The [current adopted audit](taxon_audit_adopted.json) retains earlier review fields alongside the current decisions. Use `review_confidence` for the current overall confidence, `membership_confidence` and `weight_confidence` for its components, and `adopted_groups` for the current group names, IDs and weights. Current rationale is in `reason`, `membership_source`/`membership_sources`, `weight_source` and `allocation_provenance` where supplied. The [current handoff](../coordination_handoff.json) and its latest follow-ups identify the delivered workbook and Office artifacts.

Inherited `overall_confidence`, `stored_confidence`, `stored_mapping`, `allocation_ledger`, earlier nested review objects, `adoption_state` and rule-description strings can describe a previous review. They must not override the current fields above. Thirty-two records retain an older `overall_confidence` different from current `review_confidence`; their preserved prior decisions are provenance, not an additional confidence component. For example, Ariidae now has Medium membership, Medium allocation and Medium overall confidence with groups24/25/26; its retained prior Low assessment preceded that adopted candidate correction.

All374 current component decisions satisfy the weakest-component rule. The coordinator's independent canonical-workbook comparison checks current `review_confidence`; the Excel appendix also uses it. This clarification does not change mappings, confidence decisions, scientific parameters, calculations, Office content or researcher fields.
