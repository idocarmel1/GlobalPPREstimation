# Chiaverano et al. (2018): fresh source audit

The source extraction is complete and preserves the supplied original values. The detailed source does not reproduce a balanced steady-state budget. No biological repair, canonical diet normalization, model adoption, regional workbook edit, Project.xlsx update, or map/graph change was made.

The published title is *Evaluating the role of large jellyfish and forage fishes as energy pathways, and their interplay with fisheries, in the Northern Humboldt Current System*, Progress in Oceanography 164: 28–36, DOI [10.1016/j.pocean.2018.04.009](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pocean.2018.04.009). The earlier short title is a historical alias. The focal static baseline averages **1995–1998**, rather than the cached 1995–2004 dynamic predecessor period. The source area is **165,000 km²**, extending from **4°S to 16°S and 111 km offshore** (PDF p.2, Figure 1; PDF p.3 / printed p.30, Methods). The freshly retained [Figure 1 view](evidence/pdf-page-02.png) supports geographic review, not a measured regional overlap percentage.

The original [PDF](../../../../../sources/1-s2.0-S0079661117303312-main.pdf), [XLS supplement](../../../../../sources/Supplementary material revised and final.xls), and [Word figures/tables](../../../../../sources/Peru paper figures and tables II.docx) were read afresh. Their hashes remain unchanged; [original_sources.json](evidence/original_sources.json) authenticates them. The archive README and metadata claiming no verified source are stale history and were left intact. Corrected candidate identity is in [SOURCE_IDENTITY.json](SOURCE_IDENTITY.json).

## Model structure and taxonomy

Table A contains **41 source nodes**, preserving native IDs and exact names: 36 living groups as described in prose, three detritus pools, and two fisheries nodes. The calculator representation has **39 stocks**, with producers 1–2, feeding consumers 3–35, and operational nonfeeding pools 36–39. Fleet nodes 40 and 41 remain separate Artisanal/Commercial fisheries evidence; they were not turned into biological groups with imputed biomass.

Eggs 36 are called living in prose but have blank P/B, Q/B, P/Q and AE in Table A. Table C routes a fraction 0.25 of Anchovy's detritus fate to Eggs. The prose statement that egg production rates were assumed to be 25% does not justify entering 0.25 as an annual P/B. The operational `pp=2` egg-pool classification is a disclosed translation. It is neither a recovered native classification nor an invented annual production rate.

[GROUP_IDENTITY.json](../../../../Chiaverano2018_detailed_Northern_Humboldt_1995_1998__source/extracted_tables/evidence/GROUP_IDENTITY.json), the three-column [Taxonomy.xlsx](../../../../Chiaverano2018_detailed_Northern_Humboldt_1995_1998__source/extracted_tables/evidence/Taxonomy.xlsx), and [TAXONOMY_EVIDENCE.csv](../../../../Chiaverano2018_detailed_Northern_Humboldt_1995_1998__source/extracted_tables/evidence/TAXONOMY_EVIDENCE.csv) retain membership and its scope. Native trailing spaces in Anchovy, Small hake, and Large hake were preserved. The explicitly cited [Tam et al. (2008) institutional PDF](evidence/Tam2008a.pdf), Methods pp.353–354, establishes inherited plankton sizes and species examples. Hake is *Merluccius gayi peruanus*: small <29 cm, medium 30–49 cm, large >50 cm. Printed boundary gaps and the unspecified length convention remain unresolved. No native multistanza links, transition ages, growth curves, or stanza equations were recovered; these are separately tabulated static groups.

Historical source spellings remain unchanged. Lists introduced with “e.g.” remain representative examples. Predecessor Table 1's saury, flying fish, and pelagic goby parameter donors were not promoted to local membership in Other small pelagic fish. Exact composition and numerical size limits for several residual pools remain undocumented. No stage allocation or catch weights were derived here.

## Precision and transformation checks

[SOURCE_CELLS.json](../../../../Chiaverano2018_detailed_Northern_Humboldt_1995_1998__source/extracted_tables/evidence/SOURCE_CELLS.json) links parameters, every diet/import cell, fate and catches to the exact sheet/address/source hash. [supplement_cells.json](evidence/supplement_cells.json) retains all stored XLS values, original number formats, and bold/italic styles. Stored single-precision tails add no scientific measurement precision. Most biological parameters display two decimals; the four small Chrysaora diet cells display three decimals. Positive turtle biomasses, Chrysaora EE, and some small P/Q values can display **0.00**. They were not converted to zero. Formulas versus constant-cell lineage could not be distinguished through this reader; stored values were not recalculated.

The printed biomass header includes a year denominator, although Eq.1 defines B as stock biomass. Canonical B is interpreted as t wet weight/km², with the conflicting printed header retained as evidence. No numerical time conversion was applied. P/B and Q/B are annual rates, catches are t/km²/year, and AE/EE/P/Q/diets/fates are fractions.

The separate [computational model.json](../../../model.json) contains explicit area=1, BA=0, migration=0, external-import=0 conventions and the operational egg-pool representation. [TRANSFORMATION_LEDGER.json](../TRANSFORMATION_LEDGER.json) lists all changes. BA=0 follows the declared steady-state baseline as a computational convention; it is not a numerical value recovered from a source BA table. Every known B/P/B/Q/B/EE and all canonical diet/import proportions remain native. A budget residual was never entered as recovered BA.

[Round-trip checks](../../../../Chiaverano2018_detailed_Northern_Humboldt_1995_1998__source/extracted_tables/evidence/ROUND_TRIP_CHECKS.json) pass **23,838 exact/numeric comparisons** across source cells, the imports, canonical JSON, and the reconstructed workbook. Checks include names, all canonical matrix cells, routing, source unknowns, and taxonomy. Originals were rehashed. Typed workbook cells and representative views of every exported sheet were inspected; Metadata clipping was corrected. Source JSON retains full native precision, while source formats explain its displayed precision. Fleet-node details and fate to Export remain in companions where the stock JSON format is lossy.

## Source conflicts and balance claims

[SOURCE_CONFLICTS.json](SOURCE_CONFLICTS.json) retains exact locators, competing statements, and unresolved status. The source caption's “balanced” label is an author claim, not a reconstruction result.

* **Sardine catch:** PDF p.3 / printed p.30 says landings were reduced from 5.65 to 1.4 t/km²/year to achieve balance. Native Table A J14 still contains 5.6513425. Printed Table 1's Forage fish landings 28.13 matches native Sardine+Anchovy=28.1268375, corroborating the larger native branch. A single consistent final version is not established. This candidate preserves the supplement; no 1.4 variant was run. Changing sardine catch alone cannot resolve the gelatinous production deficit.
* **Large-jellyfish diet:** Table B H4/H7/H8/H9/H39 contains 0.00985221674876847, 0.73118226600985, 0.10470443349753696, 0.0492610837438424, and 0.15, with zero import. Their sum is **1.04499999999999783**. The 4.5% excess is material, not rounding. Other totals outside 0.001 are Chub mackerel 16=0.995233899550000002, Large hake 20=1.00905, and Green turtle 34=1.002. Runtime normalization belongs to a separate diagnostic state and never alters canonical/import cells.
* **Small gelatinous production:** Table A C11/D11/E11/H11 gives B=0.009068332612514496, P/B=0.5839999914169312, Q/B=2.919999837875366, and EE=0.949999988079071. Production is about **0.00529590617 t/km²/year**. Chrysaora alone demands **23.3497540704** of this prey through Table B H9. Independent raw-diet arithmetic yields implied EE **4409.9702117**, versus source EE 0.95. Printed Small jellyfish B=0.01/PB=0.58/QB=2.92 corroborates the native magnitude. The paper's p.35 caveat calls for better small-jellyfish biomass surveys but supplies no corrected number. No magnitude repair was adopted.

[SOURCE_BUDGET_ARITHMETIC.json](../../../../Chiaverano2018_detailed_Northern_Humboldt_1995_1998__source/extracted_tables/evidence/SOURCE_BUDGET_ARITHMETIC.json) independently records production, native predation, catches, and EE disagreements. ## Aggregated alternative and scenarios

Printed Table 1 has codes 4–28: 24 stock rows plus one fleet node. ECOTRAN's production matrix adds three nutrient nodes, giving 28 production nodes. [aggregated_Table1_printed.json](aggregated_Table1_printed.json) retains exact two-decimal text, blanks, and PDF coordinates. Captions for sheets F–I are one letter later than their sheet names; both identities are recorded.

Names govern the crosswalk. Table E/ECOTRAN codes are 24=Pelagic detritus, 25=Eggs, 26=Offal; printed Table 1 has 24=Eggs, 25=Offal, 26=Pelagic detritus. Resolved egg EE=0.8843322396 differs from printed aggregate 0.87 beyond display rounding. Resolved Diatoms Table C routes detritus to Pelagic, whereas aggregate Table G sends Large phytoplankton senescence to Subsurface. Table G is a different ECOTRAN partition and does not provide an equivalent unique combined Ecopath fate matrix. Printed turtle B=0.00 loses positive native biomass. The aggregate retains both jellyfish problems and adds precision/representation conflicts, so aggregation cannot establish a repaired source model. The detailed source is preferable for membership, catch and diagnostic auditing, with limitations retained.

Scenario sheets I/II and Word Table 2 contain fractional production changes and Monte Carlo summaries, not complete independent Ecopath input sets. The focal structural scenarios use +50% jellyfish/forage consumption, +40% forage harvest, and fishery closure. They are linear asymptotic scenarios rather than separately recoverable dynamic endpoints. Neither endpoint inputs nor new scenario calculations were fabricated.
