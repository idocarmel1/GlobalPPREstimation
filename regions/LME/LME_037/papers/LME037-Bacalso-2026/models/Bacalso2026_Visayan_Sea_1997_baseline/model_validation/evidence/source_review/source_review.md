## Membership and reporting scope

The [2026 supplement](../../../../../sources/1-s2.0-S0964569125004909-mmc1-f97c887e.pdf#page=8), Table S3 on PDF pages 8–14, contains baseline-model component taxa, ecology, relative catch contributions and length traits. Main §2.3.1 on printed/PDF page 5 explicitly says additional inputs were supplied to the baseline model. The [crosswalk](supplement_membership_crosswalk.json) retains that exact passage, all 33 group identities, collapsed trait headings and exact words/positions. Traits were mainly sourced from surveys in 2004, 2009 and 2014; they are baseline membership evidence with later-survey temporal transfer. Headings combine stages 4/5, 7/8, 9/10 and 11/12; relative catch and mean/maximum size are not observed stage caught-mass fractions for 1997 or the whole-LME catch series.

Positive members include Sepia in Squids 23, Thenus and Panulirus in Other epibenthos 27, and actual family exceptions across demersal and reef pools. Source species/family representatives are not exhaustive membership boundaries for modern higher taxa. Source caught lengths are not automatic global maximum-size or habitat exclusions. The [250-label review](taxon_audit.json) records each candidate set, former mapping, taxonomy bridge, membership confidence, allocation confidence and weakest-component overall confidence; the [allocation review](allocation_evidence.json) retains all source values and genuine zero candidates.

Residual reporting keys 100039, 100139, 100239 and 100339 have verified bony ISSCAAP39 scope. Dedicated sharks 2 and rays 3 are excluded for those labels; compatible named pelagic, demersal and reef bony stocks remain eligible within explicit reporting/habitat limitations. Separate named reporting categories and provider functional tags are not exhaustive exclusions. Elasmobranchii excludes chimaeras; Chondrichthyes includes them, but the local model has no dedicated chimaera pool. That mismatch remains explicit in the broad approximation. Broad labels and unlisted oceanic, bathyal or brackish taxa retain meaningful Very low analogues rather than being represented as documented source members.

## Geography, time and interpretation

The 2023 baseline Figure 1 includes municipal waters beyond Visayan Sea proper and reports 16,728.5 km²; the sea proper is 12,521.8 km². The 2026 Figure 1 is indicative. The [geographic assessment](geography/geographic_assessment.json) traces the complete 2023 municipal/commercial envelope, registered against the 2026 graticule with consistent land exclusion and nine inside/outside water controls. Approximate coverage is 1.6% of the 1,013,866 km² Sulu-Celebes target and 98% of the reconstructed study area. Report ranges of 1–2% and 90–100% reflect coastline, envelope, registration and resolution uncertainty; the reported-area denominator is a sensitivity scenario.

Fixed 1997 coefficients and group-catch proxy mixtures applied across 1950–2019 whole-LME catches remain ecological, geographic, temporal, fleet and catch-basis assumptions. The reference year is 22 years after the baseline. Complete numerical mapping does not approve the local model for the full target. Near-singular TE and strict balance failures remain consequential even when the retained matrix has no negative entries.
