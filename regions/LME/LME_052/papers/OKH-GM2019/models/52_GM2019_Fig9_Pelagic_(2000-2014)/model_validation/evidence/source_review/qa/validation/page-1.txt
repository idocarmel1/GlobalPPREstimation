Sea of Okhotsk pelagic model validation
This record evaluates the reconstructed 2000–2014 epipelagic food web for LME 052. The native model 
balances. Its diagnostic warnings, source-number hypotheses and assumption-dependent catch mappings 
remain scientific limitations for researcher review.

the same article
Figure 9 is the generalized pelagic energy-flow network for the 2000s. Figure 10 
summarizes production by its components. Figure-only and text-plus-Figure 
calculations are reconstruction variants; the dissertation's shelf-bottom network is a 
separate supporting-source model.
Selection rationale
Article - The requested reconstruction uses the accessible whole-Sea production and 
feeding synthesis for 2000–2014, a more recent period than the previous 1980s 
model.
Model - The source-carbon Figure 9 network retains microbes and detritus and can 
be completed with the source feeding tables. The adopted variant follows the 
requested preference for plausible balance with minimal departures.
