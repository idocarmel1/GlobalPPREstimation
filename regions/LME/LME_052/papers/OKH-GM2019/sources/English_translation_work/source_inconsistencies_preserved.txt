SOURCE INCONSISTENCIES PRESERVED IN THE UNOFFICIAL ENGLISH TRANSLATION

Source: Gorbatenko & Melnikov (2019), DOI 10.26428/1606-9919-2019-198-143-163.
These observations are translation and source-fidelity notes. They do not authorize changes to the model or supply replacements for printed values.

1. Russian abstract (printed p. 143): mean annual nekton food consumption is 159 million tonnes/year. Published English abstract (p. 144): the same quantity is 159 x 10^6 tC annually. Both statements are retained.
2. The abstract and conclusion give trophic-level production III = 18.100, IV = 0.740, V = 0.016 million tC/year. Table 3 (p. 154) gives summed production III = 18.302, IV = 0.653, V = 0.015. All printed values are retained separately.
3. Table 3 calls the carbon biomass column million tC/year. The translation preserves this header while explicitly flagging /year as printed.
4. Figure 9 gives 30-60 cm pollock production as 0.59 million tC/year. Table 3 gives 0.537. Figure 9 gives trophic-level IV squid production as 0.1; Table 3 gives 0.105. These values are not reconciled.
5. Salmon P/B in the prose (p. 155) is 1.34, while the Table 3 trophic-level III salmon row gives 1.14.
6. Squid prose (p. 155) gives an annual P/B range of 2.5-3.7, averaging 3.1, then adopts 2.5. The same paragraph reports squid biomass of 2.0 million tonnes (1.2%) out of 17.254 million tonnes of nekton. The percentage is retained as printed.
7. The prose on p. 153 states 831.0 million tC/year of primary food, or 151.8 x 10^9 million tonnes wet weight. Table 3 gives 15176.0 million tonnes for the corresponding wet production total. The prose expression is preserved with a translation note.
8. The conclusion (p. 159) reports total production 1027.2 million tC, whereas the abstract, p. 157 text, and Figure 10 give 1027.4 million tC. Both totals are preserved.
9. The prose on p. 150 assigns non-predatory filter-feeding plankton to trophic level I, whereas elsewhere the source assigns these primary consumers to II. The statement is preserved with a translation note.
10. The displayed B_e equation (p. 147) prints N_1 - N_2 followed by a factor (1/2)(B_1/N_1 + B_2/N_2), without parentheses around N_1 - N_2. The translation does not insert unprinted parentheses or repair the equation.
11. Figure 9's published English caption describes underlined numbers and the next higher trophic level; the Russian caption says numbers on the lines and the subsequent trophic link. The translated caption follows Russian wording and a note preserves the English wording difference.

The original Figure 9 raster is retained without resolving its ambiguous endpoints or partly overprinted arrow labels. Its translation key translates the nodes; it does not reconstruct a diet matrix.
