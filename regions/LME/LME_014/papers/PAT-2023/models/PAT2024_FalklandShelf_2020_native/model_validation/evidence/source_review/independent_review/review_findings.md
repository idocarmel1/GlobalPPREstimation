The strongest priorities for provisional coverage are Pleoticus muelleri -> Benthic Crustaceans (benthic decapod analogue, Very low); Micropogonias furnieri and Cynoscion guatucupa/striatus -> Large and Small Demersal Fish (demersal coastal connection, Very low; both retained because no source length cutoff is supplied); Macrourus carinatus/genus/family -> Grenadier (same macrourid connection, Very low because of depth/size mismatch); Genypterus -> Kingclip (supported genus extension, Medium); Dissostichus eleginoides -> Toothfish juv (same-species analogue, Very low because adults/deep fisheries were explicitly excluded). Rajiformes/Rajidae -> Skates is a supported extension, with Rajidae's Stingrays common name/current family versus historical classification requiring specific lead review; broader non-skate-ray scope merits Very low.

Broad-category completeness requires named source pools as well as residual groups. The reviewed marine fishes proposal spans 17 source fish/shark/skate groups. The lead's marine finfishes proposal spans 14 bony-fish groups; FAO has a bony-fish reporting category, but an exact crosswalk from this SAU catch label was not established here. The pelagic proposal includes Blue Whiting, Hoki, Myctophidae, Pelagic Fish and Sharks (source Lamna nasus). Chondrichthyes/Elasmobranchii includes Dogfish, Sharks and Skates. Mollusca includes Illex, both D. gahi cohorts, Squid, Octopods and Small Zoobenthos. Cephalopoda omits the last bivalve pool; Teuthida additionally omits Octopods. Decapoda/Malacostraca/miscellaneous marine crustaceans includes Benthic Crustaceans and Zooplankton because source Munida is split between adult and juvenile compartments. Narrow shrimp/crab labels use the closer adult benthic decapod analogue. Preserve source zero catch candidates in split calculations.

The source group Small/Large Demersal Fish names do not carry the SAU <30,30-89,>=90 cm bins. Source Table 2 lists species without a quantitative cutoff. Pooled diet may help describe an analogue's limitations but does not create another eligibility test.
