# Baltic Proper Rpath port group profile

22 groups: 20 consumers, one primary producer, one detritus pool; 10 fleets. The native type-3 gear rows are retained in companions and landings/discards columns. Group IDs and source spelling are unchanged. There are no spatial prefixes. Units are native Rpath biomass density t/km² and annual rates/flows. The source ICES area is approximately SD25–29 excluding28-1; no whole-LME coverage claim is made. Report base year 2004/native fit 2004–2013; exact deposited port fitted range unverified.
