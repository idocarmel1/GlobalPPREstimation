# Baltic Proper: deposited Rpath port Ecopath snapshot

## Source identity

The sole [model.json](model.json) extracts the deposited `Rpath.parameters` and existing `Rpath.model` objects in [BalticSeaModel.RData](inputs/native/rpath_port/BalticSeaModel.RData). The [published configuration](inputs/native/rpath_port/BalticSeaModel_config.R) is retained as source text and was not executed. The original downloaded bytes and GitHub source URLs are identified in [inputs/provenance.json](inputs/provenance.json). Extraction date: 14 October 2026. Variant: **published Rpath port snapshot; materially different from the ICES accepted native configuration**.

ICES WGSAM 2016 Annex 3 (printed pp. 46–94, PDF pp. 48–96) documents the underlying Baltic key run. Table 6, printed p. 83/PDF p. 85, names native database `Baltic2004_keyrun65.eweaccdb`, Ecopath model `2000sFishModel`, EwE 6.5.14040.0. That database and the detailed Ecopath input workbook are unavailable in this package. Their acceptance does not transfer to this port. [Bauer et al. 2019](../../sources/context/Bauer_2019_future_Baltic_Sea.pdf), PDF p. 3, describes a later central-Baltic EwE model with small modifications; it does not establish identity of the downloaded Rpath bytes.

## Modeled period and area

The ICES source model has Ecopath base year **2004** and calibrated native period **2004–2013** (Annex 3 summary, printed p. 46/PDF p. 48). The downloaded port has no verified calendar-labelled fitted range. Its configuration defines `sim.years <- 500` and extends forced series; that is a simulation horizon, not evidence of a 500-year historical period or of a 2004–2014 fit. The retained model ID/year identify its source package/base-year lineage and do not certify its precise later version.

The source footprint is approximately Baltic Proper ICES subdivisions **25–29 excluding 28-1 (Gulf of Riga)**. This is a subregional model, not the entire Baltic LME. No overlap percentage or model polygon was inferred. Published stock-specific density denominators differ: e.g. adult cod 240,000 km² and adult sprat 500,000 km² (Table 1, PDF pp. 53 and 55). Native densities were not rescaled or combined with regional catches.

## Departures from the publication

There are **22 functional groups: 20 consumers, one phytoplankton producer, one detritus pool; 10 fishing fleets**, and four two-stanza fish stocks. Gear rows in the 32-row native parameter table remain fleets rather than ecological groups. Source spelling, including `SaduriaEntemon` and `PEL40OO`, is preserved.

Known deposited `Rpath.parameters` values were retained exactly. Only parameters missing there were taken from already deposited `Rpath.model` outputs, with explicit `model_estimated` roles; no new parameter was solved. This includes Mysids biomass (2.201795… t/km²), most EE values, P/Q, detritus P/B, and basal Q/B structural zeros. The raw input missing cells remain empty in [Native_parameter_grid.csv](extracted_tables/companions/Native_parameter_grid.csv); all deposited solved values remain separately identifiable in [Native_model_outputs.csv](extracted_tables/companions/Native_model_outputs.csv). Existing post-stanza juvenile B and Q/B values differ slightly from original configuration vectors; all 12 configuration/input/output differences are in [the version comparison](extracted_tables/companions/Configuration_parameter_output_differences.csv). Known Mysids EE 0.7499999 was retained even where the deposited calculated output differs.

The port differs materially from ICES Table 1. Adult sprat B is 4.48 and P/B is 1.07, against published 2.39 and 1.24; juvenile herring B/P/B/Q/B are about 1.021174/0.948/5.625844, against 1.003/1.176/5.811. Other zooplankton P/B is 30 against 20; Acartia and Temora P/B are 25 against 20. Mysids deposited model-output B differs from the native report estimate 2.16. Detritus B 1644.81 versus printed 1645 reflects differing precision. These differences were preserved, not repaired, and are recorded with page/table locators in [ICES_report_comparison.csv](extracted_tables/companions/ICES_report_comparison.csv). No researcher decision approving them has been identified.

GreySeal biomass accumulation is native absolute **0.00036 t/km²/year**, with explicit zero BA for the other 21 groups. The report specifies rate 0.06/year (Table 1, PDF p. 52), equivalent given source B=0.006. No complementary BA rate was stored. Diet and imports preserve all **460** source cells, including zeros and small source sum differences; no normalization occurred. Landings/discards preserve **440** fleet cells and their exact zero/missing distinction. Stanza links, growth, monthly age profiles, native diet producer nulls, full routing matrices, fleet fields, and configuration scenario text remain in companions.

Habitat fractions, migration and numeric off-vessel prices/fleet costs are unavailable. They were not assigned defaults. ICES describes price/cost sources at PDF p. 64, but supplies no numeric table in the retained report or native port. Whole-model density is used as the source's Rpath field basis; missing habitat fractions remain `-9999`. Five TL cells rounded through Excel by less than 5e-16 were restored to their exact deposited literals, with changes recorded in canonical `source_fidelity.restorations`.

The maintained structural validator records **one error**: native Detritus-to-Detritus fate is zero, so its standard import row sums to zero rather than the validator's required one. External fate is unavailable. This native row was preserved, without invented export or self-routing. The physical checker has **0 errors, 3 warnings** (low P/Q of the two endotherm groups and the basal zero-Q/B row); the converter reports its bounded mass-balance check as BALANCED, 0 errors, 2 warnings. These checks do not reproduce native stanza equations or establish ICES key-run equivalence.

Source→canonical checks match all retained parameter, fleet, routing and diet cells exactly. The eight imports plus 17 companion tables round trip with matching missing masks and maximum spreadsheet numeric difference 4e-16 (tolerances 1e-12 absolute, 1e-14 relative). `ModelData` loads 22 source groups plus its synthetic `diet_import` group. Its runtime alters two routing cells: phytoplankton→detritus 1 becomes 0; detritus self-routing 0 becomes 1. These loader limitations are documented in [LOADER_CHECK.json](extracted_tables/evidence/LOADER_CHECK.json); loadability is not runtime source equivalence. No calculator constructor, Ecosim, SPPR, Monte Carlo, taxon mapping, selection, regional workbook or publication stage was run.
