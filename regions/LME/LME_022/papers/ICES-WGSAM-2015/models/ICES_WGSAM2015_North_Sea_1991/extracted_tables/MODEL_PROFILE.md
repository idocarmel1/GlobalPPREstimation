# North Sea port group profile

1991 static base state; report calibration 1991–2013. ICES North Sea divisions IVa–c; exact LME overlap unmeasured. Values use the whole model area; habitat fractions remain unknown.

The deposited order comprises 65 consumer groups, Phytoplankton (66), DetritusDOMWaterColumn (67), DetritusPOMSediment (68), and Discards (69). Consumer groups include marine mammals, two seabird guilds, fish species/guilds, pelagic plankton, benthic invertebrates and microbial groups. Source spelling is preserved. Names describe group structure; exhaustive species membership was not independently extracted.

Five stocks are split into juvenile/adult stanza pairs: cod (13/14, transition 24 months), whiting (15/16, 12 months), haddock (17/18, 12 months), saithe (19/20, 36 months), and herring (28/29, 12 months). Adults are the source leading stanzas. The 11 native fleet rows are fleet evidence, not ecological groups. All growth, maturity, linkage and age-schedule fields are in [companions](companions/manifest.json).

This is the published Rpath port snapshot, which materially differs from the ICES accepted native configuration. The [model notes](../model_notes.md) identify differences and scientific limits.
