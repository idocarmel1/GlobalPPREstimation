# Mediterranean 2019 landings membership audit

The [JSON proposals](taxonomy_mapping_proposals.json) and [CSV view](taxonomy_mapping_proposals.csv) review every one of the 522 exact landings taxon labels in [LME_026.xlsx](../../../../../../../../LME_026.xlsx), including 67 labels with zero recorded 2019 catch. These are membership proposals for `Piroddi_2022_Mediterranean_1995`. Allocation weights are unassigned and owned by the regional lead. Nothing in this directory is adopted configuration or a coefficient calculation.

Membership counts are High 294, Medium 182, Low 26 and Very low 20; no label lacks a meaningful candidate. These are component grades, not final combined membership/allocation confidence or numerical SPPR coverage. Very low proposals are explicitly broad mixture approximations or habitat analogues.

The regional workbook contained no PPR / Matching rows or Selected model groups at capture. Accordingly previous membership decisions are unavailable, not assumed High. [The read-only workbook snapshot](workbook_review_snapshot.json) preserves that baseline and the provider common/functional/commercial descriptions. The exact selected [model JSON](../../../../model.json) has 71 source groups. Nonliving groups 70/71 are excluded from catch membership proposals. Missing routing remains an independent constructor blocker; no routing or biological coefficient is inferred here.

[Source group definitions](source_group_definitions.json) preserve all 71 source-group identities and Table S2 row locators. The [original source supplement](../../../../../../sources/41598_2022_18017_MOESM2_ESM-7d26163a.docx), DOCX table 2, describes species composition of the 1990s model. [Extracted DOCX table contents](source_docx_tables.json) were read directly with python-docx, and all retained definition headings with locators match those cells. Source composition is evidence of membership, not a contemporary observation of the caught-mass mixture.

The most consequential boundaries are:

- Explicit stage unions are sardine 21/22, anchovy 23/24, hake 29/30 and red mullet 37/38. Their species membership remains High. Source cutoff uncertainty belongs to allocation.
- Modern Sparidae reporting can span 25/33 because Table S2 puts Spicara in 25. Labridae spans 34/40/41, Gobiidae 40/41, Serranidae 31/34/40, Carangidae 17/19/20, catsharks 43/44/45 and drums/croakers 26/34. Whole-family composition is assumed where the provider does not retain its constituents.
- Table S2 explicitly places Chimaera monstrosa in 45, Scyllarides/Scyllarus in 60 and Squilla mantis in 59. A commercial or modern taxonomic label does not override these direct source assignments.
- Broad fish proposals include compatible named groups and residual pools. Unidentified marine fish use 13–47; groundfish and pelagic reporting use narrower but explicitly uncertain source sets. Broad cephalopods use 48/49/50; shrimp reporting retains the appropriate dedicated 53–55 and residual 56/57 pools.
- WoRMS establishes the historical Liza/Chelon, Trigloporus/Chelidonichthys, Oblada and Scomberesox nomenclatural bridges. Trisopterus minutus and source T. capelanus are distinct accepted species, so their connection is an assumed extension rather than a verified synonym.
- Source spellings `Carcharia taurus` and `Naucrates doctor` lack verified synonym entries; their obvious spelling interpretations remain M4 Medium. The corrupt source `CrassOweniidaea gigas` is retained; Magallana gigas qualifies through documented generic Bivalves containment, not a claimed exact source spelling.
- Table S2 includes Hiatella arctica in both the shark and bivalve lists, and other apparent misplaced entries. That source anomaly does not justify assigning bivalve catch to a shark group. Generic Bivalvia proposals use the explicit Bivalves compartment. Other rare misplaced source terms do not occur as exact catch labels in this universe.
- Sturgeon, pike and sea trout lack explicit source compartments and use disclosed Very low habitat analogues. Deep-water sharks use the demersal shark pool, not a Pelagic shark assignment inferred from the provider's Large sharks label.
