Tetrapturus pfluegeri
The spearfish is linked to the source billfish members in Thons majeurs, but 
is not a printed member; source-specific placement remains a weak 
analogue.
Dicentrarchus; Dicentrarchus 
punctatus
Sea bass are weak coastal/estuarine piscivore analogues. They are not 
source-listed and cannot be assigned literally to Bars, which denotes 
Pseudotolithus; exact guild boundaries remain uncertain.
Miscellaneous aquatic 
invertebrates
Actual source invertebrates include benthic taxa, euphausiids and 
unspecified larvae. Provider bins do not prove adult-only composition. 
Larval taxonomy and caught-stage composition are unobserved; source 
whole-pool catch weights are fixed across years/bases.
Centroscyllium fabricii; Oxynotus 
centrina; Scyliorhinidae
Small/medium deep sharks are weak analogues of source 
Etmopterus/Galeus. Family depth composition and group size boundaries 
remain uncertain; documented Sierra Leone occurrence prevents excluding 
Centroscyllium merely by geography.
Chimaeriformes
Chimaeras are absent from the source inventory and belong to Holocephali 
rather than sharks/rays. Deep cartilaginous pools are a weak depth/body￾form analogue; coastal and bony alternatives fit less closely. Source catch 
proportions fix an unobserved mixture across years and bases.
Cyprinidae
Freshwater carp/barbel bottom-feeding, omnivory and detritivory provide a 
weak link to shallow Mulets and coastal invertebrate feeders. The marine 
model has no freshwater compartment or measured family composition; 
source catch weights transfer those pathways across all years/bases.
Inermiidae
Bonnetmouth schooling and plankton feeding connect to the small pelagic 
guilds, but Western Atlantic identity and absence of a printed member 
weaken regional transfer. Model catch weights assume an unobserved 
coastal/offshore mixture across years and bases.
Pentheroscion mbizi

supported by 80–200 m occurrence and fish/shrimp feeding. Exact 
membership and pooled diet fit remain uncertain.
