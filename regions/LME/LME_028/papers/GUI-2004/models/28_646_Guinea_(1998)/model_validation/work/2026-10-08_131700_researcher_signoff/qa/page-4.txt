Apsilus fuscus; Brotula barbata
The snapper and brotula are weak shelf-predator analogues. Apsilus is not 
established by the source coastal Lutjanus members; Brotula also overlaps 
deeper and crustacean-feeding compartments.
Batoidea; Chondrichthyes
The four shark/ray pools lack observed species, size and habitat 
composition. Chondrichthyes additionally includes chimaeras absent from 
the source. Historical donor catch proportions mix guild members and 
reporting populations; they do not establish residual composition and are 
transferred across years/bases.
