Branchiostegus; Lagocephalus

Shelf benthic feeders are weak analogues of source members such as 
Balistes, Plectorhinchus, Pagellus and Boops. No explicit placement is 
documented; the puffer overlaps pelagic/benthic guilds and source puffers 
span several compartments.
Sarotherodon galilaeus; 
Sarotherodon melanotheron
Tilapia plant/detritus feeding links weakly to source Liza/Mugil and 
Acanthurus/Sarpa. These are not mullets or source members; 
freshwater/brackish or lagoon habitats are transferred to marine pathways 
without a freshwater compartment.
Bathygobius soporator; Gobiidae; 
Solea
Gobies and soles are weak shallow benthic-feeder analogues of the source 
coastal guild. No explicit goby compartment exists; Solea species/depth 
composition is unknown. The provider large-reef bin does not establish 
Bathygobius placement.
Miscellaneous marine crustaceans
Actual source invertebrates include benthic taxa, euphausiids and 
unspecified larvae. Provider bins do not prove adult-only composition. 
Larval taxonomy and caught-stage composition are unobserved; source 
whole-pool catch weights are fixed across years/bases.
Scorpaeniformes
Source scorpionfish, gurnard and flying-gurnard members span coastal, 
shelf and bathy groups. Legacy order boundaries and unidentified caught 
composition remain uncertain; 1998 whole-group catch weights transfer 
the mixed pathways across years/bases.

These are weak size, depth or family analogues of source large deep 
elasmobranchs; exact membership and size cutoffs are unverified. Basking 
shark follows the named whale-shark analogue despite pelagic filter 
feeding; skate catch depths and some local geographic fits remain

Centrolophidae; Peristediidae; 
Schedophilus ovalis; Schedophilus 
pemarco
Barrelfishes and armoured gurnards are weak benthopelagic-feeder 
analogues of source Ariomma or Trigla. Heterogeneous diets, uncertain 
local feeding and shelf/slope overlap leave source-specific placement 
unresolved.
Pellonula leonensis
Pellonula provides a weak small coastal-pelagic feeding analogue, but 
river/lagoon and brackish occurrence complicate transfer. Marine catch 
does not establish a shared freshwater food web or documented source 
membership.
Pseudocaranx dentex; Uraspis 
secunda
Trevally/jack are weak analogues of source Caranx/Trachinotus. Offshore 
habitat overlap and incomplete source guild boundaries prevent stronger 
membership confidence.
Holocentrus adscensionis
The reef squirrelfish is a weak nocturnal invertebrate-feeding analogue of 
source Sargocentron; exact membership and the small/medium 
compartment boundary are unverified.
