# LME_032 source evidence summary

Exact selected model: **32_1_Arabian_Sea_off_Karnataka_(2000)**. This note is based on the released [coordination handoff](coordination_handoff.json); regional Overview and Project.xlsx remain the selection authorities.

The selected Karnataka model is labelled 2000 and represents a 1999–2001 source baseline over 27,000 km² of shelf and parts of slope. The original CMFRI Bulletin PDF, source-table reconciliation, accepted canonical model and computational provenance are retained as distinct evidence. The reconciliation identifies printed basic, catch and diet tables and preserves the distinction between source-stated zeros, blanks and accepted conventions.

The current bounded review reuses adequate saved full direct GE, TE and With Egestion arrays. All three retain WARN. Rounded-source imbalance, a maximum relative production residual of 5.35%, TE PP closure gap of 3.81% and the near-singular marine-mammal group remain limitations. Finite, nonnegative matrices do not establish overall model validation. No new full extraction, solver run or global/Monte Carlo expansion is claimed by the current reuse release.

Siluriformes and Pleuronectiformes retain their exact W5 groups and weights, while allocation/overall confidence changes from Medium to Low because donor and residual reporting strata differ. Five other documented W5 lineage proxies retain Medium as regional assumptions, not measured residual composition. The mixed-guild/source-zero review explicitly considers fish-bearing and invertebrate plankton/stage pools and genuine printed-zero catches; no numerical candidate or weight change was adopted in this bounded review.

Accepted canonical bytes, raw catch, classic TL/coefficients, group inputs and arrays, annual scientific values and NPP are protected by current exact-table checks. Broad residual composition, fixed source-year/basis proxies and whole-LME transfer remain uncertain. The coordinate envelope is a locator rather than an author study polygon. Earlier source/adoption/browser records retain their historical execution identities and do not certify current shared-output reconciliation.

This summary records the regional release. Shared Project/page/browser reconciliation is coordinator-owned as recorded in the handoff. Scientific approval is not established by this note.
