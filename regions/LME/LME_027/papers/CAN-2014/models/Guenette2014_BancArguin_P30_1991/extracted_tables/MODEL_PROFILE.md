# Source taxonomy and candidate mapping profile

This review applies to Guénette, Meissa & Gascuel (2014), *Assessing the Contribution of Marine Protected Areas to the Trophic Functioning of Ecosystems*, PLoS ONE 9:e94742, and its original XLS Table 1 and DOCX File S1. The original files remain in `../../../papers/CAN-2014/`.

The Ecopath reference year is 1991; Ecosim covers 1991–2006. The modeled Mauritanian shelf is shallower than 200 m, approximately 33,224 km², including the Banc d’Arguin. It is a subset of the broader Canary Current LME. The original final model has 51 ecological groups. Computational diet import group 52 is not a source ecological group and is excluded from Taxonomy.xlsx.

`Taxonomy.xlsx` has exactly `seq`, `group_name`, and `taxon_descr`. IDs and names match original XLS Table 1. `taxonomy_evidence.json` retains original S1 spellings, source row locators, membership-list scope, life stage, habitat and source conflicts. Base, M30 and P30 share this taxonomy because the article and S8 describe perturbations of the same group structure without new member lists. Numerical scenario differences do not imply changed taxon membership.

Six fish categories have adult/juvenile pairs: meagre 3/4, croakers 14/15, seabreams 16/17, catfish 18/19, groupers 23/24 and sparids 25/26. Juveniles are age 0–1 year in the source. Numeric length thresholds are not supplied; qualitative S/M/L labels are not converted into measured size cutoffs. Banc and shelf invertebrate pools are distinct geographic compartments. Pelagic fish migrate between Morocco and Senegal and occur across the shelf; horse mackerels are benthopelagic but classed as pelagic in the source.

Source conflicts are retained. S1 has an orphan Hake row numbered 30, but final Table 1 and S2 group 30 is Octopus vulgaris. Hake is not attached to Octopus; catch hake labels use Shelf L as a Low partial-fit proxy, without asserting an undocumented model merge. S1 lists the wrasse Symphodus bailloni under Shelf selacians; that isolated entry does not justify extending all wrasses there. Source prose calls Senilia senilis a gastropod, and S6 places Sipunculida among small crustaceans. These inconsistencies do not alter the original source numbers.

All 512 exact catch labels are independently reviewed, including zero-catch labels, across catch, landings and discards for 1950–2019. Eligibility is decided from taxonomy and ecology before weights. Exact listed species or verified synonyms can have High membership; broader extensions, ambiguous mixtures and weak analogues are explicitly marked. Model numerics never raise membership confidence. M10/M11 retain meaningful approximate coverage for 68 Very low labels, with taxon-by-taxon explanations. No mapping remains unresolved, but this is not a claim of observed composition or finite Ecopath SPPR coverage.

Allocation uses W1 for a single eligible group; otherwise complete nonnegative reported Table 1 total catches with positive sum supply W4. Source-reported zeros remain explicit. Complete zero-total catch vectors fall back to W9 model biomass. No unknown source catch is converted to zero. Four taxa use W9: Echinoidea, Holothuroidea, Lepas and Paracentrotus lividus. M30/P30 inherit Base reported catch; W9 uses each scenario's own S8 biomass. Every full-precision candidate value, raw source string, ID, name, zero flag, normalization denominator and rejected catch attempt is retained in the variant evidence JSON.

Bounded authoritative regional searches were carried out before adopting model proxies. Retrieved material includes FAO length frequencies, IMROP species assessments, habitat studies and a regional shrimp-fishery discard report. No complete matching age 0–1 caught-mass vector or caught-mass allocation across the exact Banc/shelf pools was recovered. Sample number frequencies, legal sizes, nursery occurrence and biomass are not observed catch fractions. The later discard report documents young meagre and prevents interpreting source juvenile zero catches as absence across all target fleets or bases. Queries, accessed material and access failures are retained in `online_search_evidence.json`; this does not establish that better data do not exist.

Overall confidence is the weaker membership or allocation component. W4/W9 are Medium assumptions even when source numeric values are exact. Applying the same 1991 group composition to all LME years and all catch bases is an explicit temporal, geographic and fishery-basis transfer. The pre-existing selected Northwest Africa 1987 model is a different candidate; its previous High labels are not inherited.

Reference summaries use current Overview settings, 2019 landings. Catch is 5,421,499.9565191325 t wet weight. Independent simple trophic-chain PPR is 244,709,206.36682716 t C, computed using the frozen Classic PPR taxon coefficient and dividing wet-weight carbon conversion by nine exactly once. All 210 year/basis sums reconcile with saved classic annual totals. The 100 unavailable TL/coefficient labels all have zero reference landings; they contribute zero annual PPR while coefficients remain unavailable. Unrounded numeric values drive sorting and all shares.

Overall High/Medium/Low/Very low/Unresolved counts are 116/283/45/68/0. Their reference catch shares are 49.45845067718626%, 24.964551883190833%, 1.745599369941106%, 23.831398069681796%, and 0%. Their simple-chain PPR shares are 37.2606579408101%, 39.3882582696753%, 9.774965237253566%, 13.576118552261049%, and 0%. Mapping resolution is 100%; assumption-dependent catch is 50.541549322813744%. These mapping summaries are equal across the three variants; numerical W9 weights can differ.

This is a candidate-specific supporting handoff. It does not adopt model selection, modify the regional workbook or its classic results, or determine candidate GE/TE/Egestion coefficients. Those calculations are produced separately from each exact variant. Local workbook and source identities are retained in the evidence inventory.
