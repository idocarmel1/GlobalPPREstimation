BALANCING THE MODEL
The unbalanced model for Northwest Africa is shown in Table 16. In order to obtain a balanced solution,
different levels of verification have been made. First, it was important to make sure that gross efficiency
(GE), which is the ratio of production to consumption (P/Q) was always within the 0.1 – 0.3 range.
According to Christensen and Pauly (1992), GE ranges between 10 and 30%, with the exception of top
predators, e.g., marine mammals and seabirds, which can have lower GE (between 0.1 and 1%), and small,
fast growing fish larvae or nauplii or bacteria, which can have higher GE (between 25% and 50%)
(Christensen and Pauly 1992).
