## Membership and reporting scope

Every label, including previous High and zero-catch labels, was independently assessed. Source-explicit memberships remain High. Table1a lists Carangidae in Miscellaneous piscivores without a Primarily qualifier, so its existing single-group High is retained. Current Istiompax indica and Kajikia audax are source Makaira indica and Tetrapturus audax, verified through NCBI and WoRMS; source-union membership remains High while stage allocation is Medium. Shortbill spearfish extends the listed marlin representatives at Medium; Isurus extends source I.oxyrinchus to an unseparated mako genus at Medium. Istiophoridae spans both marlin and sailfish stages, excluding swordfish/Xiphiidae. Scombridae includes the named tuna/Auxis/wahoo groups and the Euthynnus/Sarda-bearing piscivore and Scomber/Scomberomorus-bearing epipelagic guilds. The mixed pools contain other taxa, so their catch composition is an explicit proxy assumption.

Marine/pelagic unidentified finfish use all22 represented finfish compartments, including flyingfishes30 and mesopelagic32. The finfish interpretation is an assumption supported by provider categories and the IATTC Osteichthyes reporting convention, not observed SAU constituent composition. Sharks/rays/skates use both shark stages and the source Manta pool; absent benthic elasmobranchs and other rays/skates limit membership. Southern bluefin uses the sole Pacific bluefin group as a Very low analogue, with FAO cold-temperate southern distribution and residency mismatch disclosed. Its recorded zero landings and small nonzero total catch remain untouched.

Group35 contains larval fishes within a mainly zooplankton guild. It is excluded under an explicit commercial-finfish reporting/stage assumption because its pooled plankton biomass is not a defensible composition proxy for these labels. This is neither a measured gear exclusion nor a claim that larvae cannot be caught. All39 source groups have an include/exclude decision for every taxon in [candidate decisions](candidate_decisions.csv); organism-type and competing-guild exclusions remain visible. No basal group receives living catch. All four Very low labels and their exact candidates are named in the appendix; none remains unresolved.

## Stage allocation

The [online search](online_search.json) checked primary IATTC data and the 2019 fisheries report before applying proxies. Length histograms, gear-specific observer samples and whole-EPO distributions do not establish all-fleet HS_077 caught-mass fractions at the80/90/150cm cutoffs. No applicable observed mass fraction was recovered; this is a bounded search result, not proof that raw sampling data do not exist. Length type and exact-cutoff equality are unspecified in Table1a.

Existing12 stage splits retain exact accepted source-total-removal proportions. Additional marlin/sailfish/shark and supported mixed-family sets use complete finite nonnegative canonical source exports from the five-fleet landings-plus-discards tables, normalized only over the reviewed eligible candidates. A species/genus inside a pooled group inherits that pool's ratio by assumption. Model total removals are transferred to regional landings and discards separately, fixed across1950–2019. The paper's two Dolphin fleet catches are censored below0.01t/million km2/year; canonical numeric totals omit them and the limitation is retained.

Flyingfishes30 and mesopelagic32 have all-blank fisheries rows in Tables5a/6a, canonical export -9999, and runtime zero. They are unknown source catches, not observed or explicitly assumed uncaught zeros. Thus the broad-finfish catch proxy fails completeness; both labels use all22 candidates' complete model biomass at Medium allocation confidence. The source raw values, rejected catch attempt, total, exact proportions and assumptions are retained in the workbook Allocation assumptions and [audit](taxon_audit.json). Every candidate is retained; no candidate was dropped to obtain a denominator. The one explicit source0.00 Small swordfish fleet cell is distinguished from missing cells and from its positive other-fleet group total.
