## Taxonomy and geographic admission

`taxonomy.csv` covers all 39 canonical groups, in canonical sequence and exact group names, using Table 1a (printed pp.152–153 / PDF22–23); detritus is documented by Table 3a. Source spellings and the qualifiers “Primarily” are preserved. The table is group composition, but its caption cautions that common names need not list every species. It does not establish catch allocation weights. `matching_review_NOT_ADOPTED.csv` inventories all 26 catch labels. Skipjack, albacore, Pacific bluefin and Carangidae have explicit membership in one group. Yellowfin, bigeye, sharks, swordfish, dorado and other split taxa need regional size allocation. Neither model biomass nor its 1993–1997 fishery totals establish 2019 HS_077 size weights. Higher taxa, unsupported species and unverified modern synonyms remain unresolved. No living catch is assigned to a basal group.

The model covers the pelagic ETP, approximately 32.8 million km² between 20°N and 20°S, 150°W and the American shelf break. It is broader than the HS_077 high-seas catch unit. Metadata's intended-envelope target ratio is not a measured geographic fraction. Fixed 1993–1997 coefficients, even if later admitted, would be an explicit regional/time extrapolation.
