MEMBERSHIP 2 Seabirds(Phoebastria nigripes, Phoebastria immutabilis, Diomedea spp.) 
PROSE 6 Discards
PROSE 8 In the absence of dietary studies of seabirds in the Warm Pool, used diet composition (by % weight) of six species of Diomedeids in the North Pacific Ocean (Hawaii), which was dominated by epipelagic squids (20-67%), fishes (9-50%), and crustaceans (5-40%) to a lesser extent migratory mesopelagic fishes, crustaceans and fishery discards. Many of these species migrate across the entire Pacific Ocean, so these diets are likely to be representative in the Warm Pool at the functional group level.

PROSE 310 Detritus
PROSE 310 There are no recent biomass estimates for detritus in the open ocean of the Warm Pool, apart from those of Emerson et al. (1987) for the abyssal plains of three open ocean regions, which includes the equatorial Pacific. These ranged from 53-103 t km-2. Given the Warm Pool is oligotrophic, we used the minimum estimate (53 t km-2) as an initial value, which did not require manipulation during balancing.
