3.3 
Model inputs and balancing the model 
Unless otherwise stated, the unassimilated food fractions were set to default value of 0.2 
(Winberg 1956) for all groups, assuming that 80% of the consumption is physiologically 
useful while the 20% unassimilated part (consisting of urine and faeces) is directed to the 
detritus. Winberg's 20% estimate was found to be generally accurate for predatory fishes 
(Brett and Groves 1979). 
3.3.1 
Primary producers 
In this model, there were two functional groups of primary producers: phytoplankton and 
benthic producers.
