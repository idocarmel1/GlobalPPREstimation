# Validation artifacts and human decisions

Each retained model owns `model_validation/validation.docx`, `taxon_mapping.xlsx`, and necessary evidence. Its extraction row links to adjacent `model_notes.md` and summarizes supported departures. Preserve researcher text, deleted prose, manual cells, formatting, signatures, confidence rules and diagnostic findings when rebasing links.

The [validation skill](../tools/skills/ecopath-model-validation/SKILL.md) and [template guide](../tools/templates/instructions.md) govern scientific/report checks. Moving or relinking a report does not renew approval. Existing review records retain their scientific identity and freshness checks. Local links resolve relative to the containing Office file; external links and internal anchors remain intact.

Loadability, numerical health, readiness and researcher approval remain separate. Constructor failures and unavailable matrices are recorded, never repaired merely for layout compliance. See [structure.md](structure.md) for work/output destinations and [the execution record](plans/project_reorganization_plan.md#14-execution-record) for performed checks.
