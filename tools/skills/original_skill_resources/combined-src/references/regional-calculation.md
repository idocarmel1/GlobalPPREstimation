## Broader mapping definitions and confidence

Sort the report's group-assignment rules by unrounded PPR coverage, highest first. Add a Sum row only if the unrounded total differs from 100% beyond numerical tolerance (1e-8 percentage points), explaining the discrepancy. Omit it when the total is 100%. The Word template and filling guide use the same rule.

Prefer approaching 100% catch coverage with transparent, meaningful approximations over leaving taxa unresolved to preserve certainty. This preference applies to the whole mapping stage, including broad taxonomic labels and geographic/habitat gaps, not only size/stage splits. It does not change source-model definitions or establish model validity.

- **Broad-category approximation (M10, Very low):** when an unidentified or high-level label has uncertain containment across compartments, review compatible named groups as well as residual pools. Record included/excluded candidates and allocate an assumed mixture across that set. A broad rank alone does not require M10: a documented coherent regional taxon or restricted reporting category can qualify for stronger membership rules. Unknown constituent species, incomplete strict containment or mixed model pools do not alone require Unresolved. Explain any coarse catch functional classification used or set aside.
- **Closest represented analogue (M11, Very low):** when only a weak but meaningful taxonomic/ecological connection to represented groups can be established, use the closest group or group set. State the actual depth, habitat or taxonomic mismatch and why the analogy is usable. Absence of a named species compartment or offshore occurrence alone is insufficient if an explicit generic guild fits; keep broader model applicability separate. A poorly represented deep-water assemblage may require a pelagic analogue, while a broad cartilaginous-fish label may use shark/ray pools with unrepresented chimaeras disclosed. These illustrate reasoning, not universal assignments.
- **Very low** is below Low and above Unresolved. Record membership and weighting confidence separately; overall confidence is the weaker component in **High > Medium > Low > Very low > Unresolved**. A one-group analogue has weight 1 and High allocation confidence but remains Very low overall. Supported partial/conflicting evidence may remain Low; preserve stronger existing decisions.
- **Source geographic allocation (W10, High allocation):** applicable source quantities describe caught mass of the relevant taxon across areas corresponding to the exact model compartments. Normalize those quantities after recording boundaries, period, catch basis, units and group correspondence. Geographic range overlap or visual area overlap alone is not measured catch composition; transferred or approximate data receive lower confidence.
- If direct composition evidence is unavailable, use reviewed candidate model-catch proportions, then complete model-biomass proportions, at Medium allocation confidence. If neither works, a justified expert numerical rule, including an explicitly justified equal-share assumption, may use **W11 Very low**. Retain every candidate, raw value, genuine zero, rejected attempt, proportion and transfer assumption. See [allocation evidence and calculations](size-stage-allocations.md).
- **Unresolved** is reserved for no meaningful candidate/analogue, evidence contradicting every candidate, or no justifiable numerical allocation even under W11. Never choose an unrelated group solely to reach 100%, fabricate observed composition, or fill missing SPPR/TL with invented values. Missing coefficients are a separate limit on numerical coverage.

Keep source membership in `taxon_descr` faithful to the paper. Store broader assumed membership, weight basis, confidence and reasons in the regional mapping review/assumption ledger; never rewrite source taxonomy to present an analogue as a reported member. Compute catch coverage using finite coefficients for the actual scope/method/year/basis. Report the Very low catch share and known independent classic-PPR share alongside total coverage. In the validation report, briefly group and flag Very low cases, link exact taxa/weights in the appendix, and name unresolved taxa with their reasons or state none. Keep skill changes, integration history and verification logs in supporting evidence.

Exact/synonym matching is the first pass. Review high-tonnage unresolved labels for source-supported aliases and containment. If all supported constituents belong to one group, within-pool proportions are unnecessary; if they span groups, establish measured or explicitly assumed membership and weights. Distinguish representative from exhaustive lists, missing model structure from spelling issues, and taxon labels from species. Find missing evidence using [online recovery](missing-data-recovery.md), then assess M10/M11 if a meaningful broader placement remains possible. A group name alone does not establish High confidence.

For a full confidence reassessment, follow the [taxonomy reassessment procedure](group-taxonomy.md#reassessing-membership-confidence) and the validation guide's component criteria. Inspect prior High/Medium decisions as well as weak ones; use provider reporting meaning, relevant regional members and historical taxonomic concepts. Separate uncertainty about membership in a group pair from its internal stage weights. Record old/new decisions for every taxon and keep confidence-only adoption separate from group/weight proposals. Numerically reproduced model proportions remain Medium assumptions unless stronger applicable allocation evidence is recovered.

**Nearby-model fallback:** when no better mapping can be made from the focal model and taxon evidence, it is permissible to use mappings from geographically close models. Inspect the donor's evidence, ecological setting and group definitions, then map compatible concepts to the selected model's exact group IDs. Record the donor model/version/area, taxon assignment, source locator, crosswalk, geographic/ecological differences and transfer rationale. Assess confidence for the receiving mapping; do not copy the donor rating automatically. A supported transfer can justify Medium membership, a partial fit Low, and a weak but meaningful analogue Very low. It does not become explicit focal-source membership or High solely through proximity. Allocate among the receiving model's eligible groups using the normal evidence hierarchy; donor weights need their own applicability assessment. Do not import donor SPPR or replace the selected model as part of this fallback.

For a taxon split by size, age, life stage or cohort, use [size-stage-allocations.md](size-stage-allocations.md). Search for regional caught-mass composition online before a model-catch proxy. An explicitly authorized, recorded assumption can resolve a stage split or broader M10/M11 placement provisionally; taxonomy/geography uncertainties remain in the confidence and rationale. One selected model-region pair owns one mapping, reused across retained years and bases with that extrapolation documented. Preserve source diagnoses and separate assumption-dependent catch coverage from total mapped coverage.

Report resolved labels and catch-tonnage coverage by year and basis, with the largest unsupported catches. Geographic overlap is separate. Label supported-catch subtotals as partial; fixed-period coefficients over historical catch do not reconstruct annual ecosystems. For PPR differences, separate catch quantity/composition, coefficients, scope, mapping support and feedback. High positive SPPR or a lower PPR alone is not an accuracy criterion; use diagnostic/source evidence rather than invented universal cutoffs.
