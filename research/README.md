# Research studies

Placement follows purpose and ownership. `human/` contains researcher-owned experiments and method comparisons; `agents/` contains supporting audits, baselines, reviews and integration evidence. Agent assistance does not change study ownership. Neither category implies scientific approval.

Each study remains one complete package, with its existing inputs, code, results and evidence. Historical statements, manifests and execution records retain their original meaning and recorded paths. Resolve old paths through [the source disposition ledger](../common_reference_data/provenance/source_paths.csv), rather than executing frozen scripts against live data.

Current scientific inputs, results and validation reports remain model-local under `regions/`. New temporary preparation runs use the work locations in [the project contract](../explainers/agents/project_contract.md).
