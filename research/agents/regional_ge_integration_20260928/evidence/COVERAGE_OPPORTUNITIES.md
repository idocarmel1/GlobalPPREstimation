# Coverage gained by resolving size and life-stage allocations

These are conditional opportunities calculated from the project's 2019 total catch (landings plus discards). They are not newly adopted mappings. Actual size/stage proportions require evidence appropriate to the region and year; diagnostic and model applicability problems remain even after mapping is resolved.

## HS077 — Eastern Central Pacific high seas

Total catch: 860,671.2076 tonnes. Currently covered: 415,664.8921 tonnes (48.2954%).

| Allocations resolved | Added covered catch (tonnes) | Resulting coverage | Gain (percentage points) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Yellowfin | 173,477.03 | 68.45% | 20.16 |
| Yellowfin and bigeye | 297,307.47 | 82.84% | 34.54 |
| All 12 currently identified size-only gaps | 380,516.40 | 92.51% | 44.21 |

The last scenario includes wahoo, Alopias, silky shark, oceanic whitetip shark, Coryphaena, common dolphinfish, sailfish, blue shark, Sphyrna, yellowfin, bigeye and swordfish. It excludes labels with additional membership or synonym-reconciliation issues. The remaining 7.49% therefore cannot be resolved through these size allocations alone.

The coverage gain is independent of the particular small/large mixture provided every component receiving catch has a usable coefficient and the weights sum to one. The mixture changes PPR, potentially substantially. An assumed all-large mixture would create the same nominal coverage but would not provide the same evidential support.

## Priority and evidence

The immediate priority is yellowfin/bigeye for HS077 and skipjack/yellowfin/bigeye for the three WCPO regions. Retrieve catch-size or life-stage composition, stratified by fishery/gear, geography and year, and convert numbers at size into catch-mass fractions. Do not infer caught-fish sizes from a broad taxonomic functional-group label such as “large pelagics”.

See [size-allocation research](SIZE_ALLOCATION_RESEARCH.md) for primary online sources and the proposed allocation calculation. Detailed arithmetic is retained in HS077_size_allocation_coverage_potential.json and WCPO_tuna_allocation_coverage_potential.json. HS077 evidence uses the current provisional summary and the source-supported candidate-group audit; its catch totals were checked against the summary. WCPO figures were calculated directly from each regional workbook's Catch table and checked against the regional integration summaries.
