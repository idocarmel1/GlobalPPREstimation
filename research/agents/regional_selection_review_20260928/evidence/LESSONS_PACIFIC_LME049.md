# Retained-evidence lessons: Pacific candidates and LME049

Reviewed 28 September 2026. This is a retrospective **review of retained reports and verification artifacts**, not firsthand execution of these extractions. My firsthand pipeline work was LME024; similarities noted here are comparisons, not claims that I ran the Pacific/LME049 work. No extraction, scientific calculation, selection, central update or skill edit was performed for this review.

The main lesson is to preserve separate answers to five questions: was the source recovered, was it transcribed faithfully, what did conversion/loading change, did the requested calculation succeed scientifically, and what exact variant did the user select? These cases demonstrate that none of those answers implies the next.

## 1. Source recovery and model identity

**Griffiths2019:** initial publisher requests returned challenge HTML/403; the author-upload text parser exposed numbers but collapsed blank cells, making predator/fleet assignments unsafe. Bounded HTTP range recovery, Content-Range checks, title/page validation and hashes established complete main PDFs. The later user-supplied original DOCX superseded the supplement retrieval blocker. Its final S3 diet and S4 catch tables were independently checked against a read-only Word PDF render: 567 nonzero diet cells and 172 nonblank catch cells matched coordinates. Initial S2 was correctly excluded. The old retrieval findings remain in history rather than being overwritten.

**Allain2021:** a complete four-page report and a separate 24-page context report were recovered, but neither supplied the final 65-group input set. An older EcoBase model with a similar regional name was not substituted. Complete download and complete model evidence are different states.

**Watari2019:** the 46-page supplement was identified and partially exposed by a web reader, but exact-byte retrieval failed with HTTP401/bot checks. The retained reports therefore do not claim a complete archived supplement or exhaustive pooled taxonomy. **Chen2025:** all five DOCX tables were read as OOXML, but no page-rendered check was completed because LibreOffice was unavailable. That is a disclosed verification limitation, not a claimed visual pass.

Suggested rules: reject HTML/partial files as source artifacts even when a download has a plausible filename; preserve retrieval status separately from scientific admission; never assign numeric table cells from flattened prose when blank positions are lost; reopen a blocked extraction when a new source arrives and retain the old status as history. Add a documented Windows read-only Word rendering fallback when appropriate—the Griffiths workflow demonstrates one, and it also worked firsthand for LME024.

## 2. Conversion must be audited, not trusted as a transcription step

WCP2007's converter normalized diets, inserted habitat_area=1, dropped P/Q and TL, and omitted an entirely blank initial Detritus row. The source-restored canonical retained 24 initial groups while the converter reconstruction had only23. Exact diets, missing fields, output/input flags and omitted rows were restored with a changes audit. The engine-named JSON was an identical-byte loader alias, not another model.

Watari prints both habitat-area biomass and total-model-area biomass. The canonical uses the printed whole-area values, avoiding extra digits manufactured by multiplying rounded habitat values. Pooling sums the whole-area detritus biomasses **9.58+6.04+28.50=44.12** without multiplying habitat fractions again. Griffiths S4 catch values require exactly one **10⁻⁶** multiplier; they must not then be divided by area. WCP catches are already density rates. Griffiths' main and appendix derivations also use different areas; those discrepancies were documented without silently rescaling final densities.

Suggested rules: compare extraction, converter output, source-restored canonical and post-loader state as distinct artifacts; check group count, complete identifiers, exact diet cells, sentinels, units and input/output flags at every boundary. Require an explicit unit equation for each conversion and a warning against applying area twice. A round-trip confirms representation fidelity only; it cannot validate an inconsistent source model.

Evidence: [WCP final extraction and converter audit description](../../../regions/EEZ_941/models/941_200701_WCPO_Warm_Pool_Final_(mixed_periods)/extracted_tables/REPORT.md); each LME049 model's `extracted_tables/converter_transformations_reversed.json`; the Watari and Griffiths reports cited above.

## 3. Parameter precedence, censoring and unresolved native model structure

WCP2007 has separate initial24-group and final31-group parameterizations, not one interchangeable table set. Specific final GS values override the general0.2 prose default. Tuna BA/B=0 is explicit; other BA is unknown. The catch table uses the earlier roster and does not explicitly establish the final native catch state. The period is genuinely mixed, so the archive avoids inventing a single year from stock-assessment publication dates. Native multistanza settings are not fully represented by standalone group rows.

Watari is **one linked spatial model**, not three independent regional models. Its seabird biomass and five catches printed `<0.01` remain censored unknowns, not zero or half-bound values. Chen's54 `'+'` diet entries remain censored; the caption's percentage wording was not silently converted to exact fractions. Main species-composition tables were distinguished from citations to studies used for diets. Source spellings were retained, and Chen's inherited author-name error was corrected from the article.

Suggested rules: establish the table/version/name spine first; distinguish explicit numeric values, bounded values, structural blanks and unknowns; do not infer missing routing from habitat fractions or prey composition; keep native multistanza limitations visible. A preferred paper is not an exact model selection, and an initial/final roster mismatch needs an explicit mapping and caveat.

## 4. Pooling is an assumption variant, not recovery of missing routing

The original Griffiths46-group model cannot load because the natural-mortality/egestion split between its two detritus pools is missing. The authorized45-group pooled variant preserves consumer inputs and sums only the two detritus prey fractions and biomasses. It loads, but all three direct diagnostics return FAIL. Its loader replaces source detritus BA=0 with approximately416.710466; a run is therefore not an exact steady-state reconstruction. Pooling also does not introduce native fleet-discard returns: gross catch still includes discarded removals, while the natural fate matrix routes M0+egestion.

Watari's original41-group model is similarly blocked by missing fate among pools39–41. The authorized39-group variant retains all35 consumers and all three phytoplankton groups, sums detritus diets exactly, and leaves pooled EE unknown rather than averaging it. It assumes cross-block mixing and full retention through the single-pool default. That assumption is not supplied by habitat fractions0.2/0.2/0.6.

Suggested rules: pooling requires its own identity, original-to-derived group map, changes-only audit and preserved original. Aggregate compatible stocks and flows; do not average EE; verify unchanged consumer totals. Audit fishery removals and internal discard returns separately. Record the original model's NOT_RUN independently of the pooled experiment's results. These are consistent with the existing extraction and mortality/discard principles; an explicit pooling checklist would make them harder to overlook.

## 5. Post-loader changes can dominate an apparently good diagnostic

The Watari completed runtime changes censored seabird B<0.01 to **B=1**, and its printed PB0.12/QB36.67 to zero. The retained report calls these unresolved loader artifacts. Seventeen missing catches become zero, including five censored catches. Unknown BA is solved: pooled detritus BA becomes **+1021.626599**, approximately23.16 times standing biomass annually. Near-zero completed equation residuals consequently do not establish source steady state.

Chen's censored diet sentinels become zero and twelve consumer columns are normalized; catches, GS and unknown flows are completed. WCP's loader derives BA for24 source groups and turns a detritus surplus into BA≈208.048, whereas the source describes unused detritus as sedimentation export. These are scientifically material representation choices.

Griffiths' first pooled exact-diet run also failed before returning diagnostics because missing synthetic-import cells produced NaNs in basal identity detection. The retained normalize_DC=True path resolved the computational issue, with all diet changes logged. This is independently similar to the four-basal-cell failure I traced firsthand in LME024; it is not evidence that the two runtime conventions were identical.

Suggested addition: make a post-initialization source-to-runtime audit a required gate. Flag real-group biomass imputation, censor-to-zero conversion, known-BA replacement, derived surplus accumulation, diet normalization and overwritten rates prominently. Defaults may support explicitly conditional experiments; they must never silently become empirical source values. In particular, the documented Watari B=1 artifact should be treated as a lesson against future silent biomass filling, not as an endorsed completion rule.

That distinction prevented an incorrect second-cause story. Suggested rule: decompose global PP imbalance into weighted group residuals when the existing equations support it; verify parser, units, normalization and source version before biological changes. Report source arithmetic and loaded arithmetic separately.

**Conditional BA closure explains the contrast between the pooled runs.** Watari publishes no numeric BA, and its runtime solves BA throughout the model, so GE/Egestion input-balance success is conditional on those new residual flows. Griffiths explicitly states BA=0 for all source groups. Its runtime replaces detritus BA but leaves the major living-group source contradiction, including Pomfret, exposed; all three outcomes remain FAIL. Therefore the retained evidence does not show that Watari's original sources are intrinsically better balanced merely because its pooled GE/Egestion grades are OK. The source constraints and completion freedoms differ. No retained test in this review establishes how either model would behave under an alternative BA convention.

**WCP rounding versus unresolved native accounting can be partly distinguished.** Normalizing the printed diets changes juvenile bigeye predation by approximately−0.0000000362712 and juvenile yellowfin by−0.00000848504. The maximum production residual changes only from69.4228% to69.4210%, so rounding does not explain the failure. Exact fleet sums, table coordinates and units were independently checked. The weighted-residual equality rules out an additional unexplained solver/global-budget discrepancy in that audited runtime; it does not prove that the standalone engine reproduces native EwE multistanza accounting. The cause report explicitly leaves native stanza transfers, catch-state mismatch and publication error unresolved. The later D_fixed_M0 variant changes small BET PB0.834→1.4129713563375232 and EE0.644→0.7898725981469387; small YFT PB1.983→2.5304972811160384 and EE0.849→0.8816702937266387, preserving original other-mortality flows. ## 7. Coordination, reporting scope and latest status

The retained LME049 work first ran a broad22-method/MC inventory; later reports were narrowed to the requested direct diagnostics and the broad evidence was archived. That history supports asking what decision the diagnostic is meant to inform, then using only the requested configurations. Execution status `ok`, diagnostic grade OK, strict balance true, scientific admission and selection are separate fields. Preserve raw full returns, exceptions and NOT_RUN records; do not narrate an unavailable method as a failed numeric result. Do not drop negative coefficients in unfished groups.

Older Pacific comparison/index documents say “no selection,” but later retained selection records supersede that status: the user selected **WCP option1/D_fixed_M0** for HS_071, EEZ_941 and EEZ_598, and the **39-group Watari pooling variant** for LME049. Those records retain limitations and pending regional calculations. This review did not reselect or approve them. Resolve current decisions from dated decision/verification records rather than repeating stale summaries or erasing history.

Spatial comparisons use actual regional polygons and distinguish the fraction of the target covered from the fraction of the broad model lying in the target. WCP and Griffiths cover almost all EEZ_941 but remain broad pelagic proxies; coverage does not establish coastal/reef fit. Shared paper identity across regions avoids duplicate extraction, but does not establish identical applicability. Do not rank the models by returned total PPR when areas, catches, periods and structures differ.

Central coordination had a concrete failure: an empty duplicate Models row42 obstructed selection lookup, and atomic workbook replacement was denied. The staged patch was not a license to overwrite a changed workbook. Later verification records a narrowly scoped removal of a valueless row, with all other package parts byte-identical, followed by successful consolidation and unrelated-record checks. The old blocked record is therefore historical, not the current status.

Suggested rules: one central writer at a time; fresh hash/re-read before applying a staged update; rebuild a narrow change when the live hash differs; retain backups and verify unrelated rows/tables; never force-close applications to work around an unidentified lock. Record exact variant and tested hash at selection, then separately track central synchronization and pending calculation.

## Priority for a later skill revision

Source preservation, visual/cell checks, exact units, separate experimental variants and mortality/discard distinctions are existing principles that these reports reinforce. The highest-value operational additions are: **(1)** a mandatory post-loader transformation audit with critical biomass/BA/censor flags; **(2)** explicit source/converted/runtime/selected-state labels and latest-decision precedence; **(3)** a small basis/NaN troubleshooting check before interpreting solver exceptions biologically; **(4)** a pooling-and-fishery-return accounting checklist; **(5)** a scoped diagnostic contract and weighted-residual explanation before any proposed biological repair. These are recommendations from retained evidence, not an exhaustive audit of the current skill text or changes already applied to it.
