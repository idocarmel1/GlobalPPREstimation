Table S3-1. Overview of parameters for multi-stanza groups for the balanced models for year 1950. K is the growth coefficient of the von Bertalanffy growth 
equation, Recruitment power is a parameter to adjust the recruitment –spawning stock relationship, BA is biomass accumulation, i.e. change in biomass from 
one year to another, Wmat is weight at maturity and Winf is the weight at infinity (infinite age). Default Wmat/Winf is 0.09. Leading group means that parameters 
(B and Q/B) for other stanza of the multistanza group are adjusted according to the values of the leading group (stanza). Transition age (Tr. Age) is transition 
age (month) from one stanza to another.
